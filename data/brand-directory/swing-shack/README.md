# Swing Shack — Brand Directory One-Pager

## What this brand is
Premium indoor golf experience in Johannesburg. TrackMan bays, club fittings, coaching,
memberships. Data-driven, evidence-based, no-nonsense.

## Who it is for
Golfers aged 28–55 in the greater Johannesburg area who care about getting better,
not just looking good. They are sceptical of hype. They respect data.

## What it is not
- Not a generic golf entertainment venue — we are a performance facility.
- Not a "buy clubs off the rack" store — we fit.
- Not aspirational/luxury copy — we are practical and credible.
- Not for first-timers or weekend-only golfers — they are welcome, but not the core.
