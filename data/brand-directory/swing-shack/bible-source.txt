SWING SHACKFull Brand Bible  |  Prepared for the Swing Shack team  |  Draft v1  |  For staff training and day-to-day behaviourThis document is how Swing Shack shows up — in what we say, how we behave, and how we make people feel. It is a guide for every member of the team, not just the marketing crew. If you are ever unsure how to act or speak, come back to this.1. Brand Foundation — What Swing Shack IsSwing Shack is South Africa's first and only true indoor golf club. It is a high-performance, data-driven, coach-led facility using TrackMan and the Zen Swing Stage. We combine coaching, club fitting, practice, and social play under one roof.We are not a driving range. We are not an arcade with golf screens. We are not a country-club extension. We are a performance environment where serious golfers come to get better, and where every visitor — beginner or scratch player — is treated like a member.One line: Real Golf, Indoors. No guesswork. No stuffy club vibes. Just your game.2. Brand EssenceBetter golf, built on real data and a real welcome. Every golfer is unique — physically, mentally, emotionally. Their clubs, their practice, and their coaching must be tailored to them, not the other way around.The core idea: "Your swing isn't generic, so why settle for generic golf?" Whether it is a fitting, a coaching session, or a practice block, we make it personal, measurable, and tied to real outcomes.3. Brand PurposeTo help every golfer play their own best golf — through coaching that uses data to cut through vague advice, fitting that treats clubs as pure science for your swing, and a club experience that makes getting better feel good.4. Brand MissionGive serious golfers a world-class performance hub: coaching, fitting, practice and play, all in one place.Make improvement measurable and exciting — data-driven, not guesswork.Fit and build clubs to a player's real swing, using ClubLab-built precision.Welcome every level — from first-time beginners to aspiring tour players — without condescension.Build a community where serious golfers train, compete, and belong (leagues, the Combine, the membership).5. Brand VisionTo be Johannesburg's — and ultimately South Africa's — most respected indoor golf hub: the place all players choose to come to get fitted, get coached, and be part of a real indoor golf club.6. Brand Promise"The ball speaks. Data confirms. Feel seals the deal."Everything we do must honour that line. We show the truth through data, we let the golfer feel the truth, and only then do we talk about the next step. No one leaves wondering what happened or why.7. Brand Values — The Non-Negotiables7.1 Data-Driven, Not GuessworkTrackMan tells the truth. We use real-time data — launch, spin, dispersion, strike, carry — to make decisions and to show golfers why something works. No vague advice, no 'feel my way through it.' Every recommendation has a number behind it.7.2 Player-FirstWe fit clubs to the player, never the player to the club. If the answer is not the brand they walked in wanting, we say so honestly. We do not push price, prestige, or margin.7.3 Craftsmanship &amp; StandardsA fitting only works if the built club matches the fitted spec. Loft, lie, length, swing weight, shaft, grip — every detail. We are a ClubLab fitment centre and our builders treat the workshop like a lab. The Swing Shack Standard applies to the workshop, the bays, and the welcome.7.4 Warmth &amp; WelcomeSwing Shack is a club, not a clubhouse with a velvet rope. Whether a golfer is a tour aspirant or holding a club for the first time, they get the same greeting, the same respect, and the same care. Serious golf, minus the snobbery.7.5 Improvement That Feels GoodBetter golf is exciting, not intimidating. We celebrate progress, we make practice rewarding, and we make the numbers feel like a win — because for our golfers, they are.8. Brand PositioningCategory: High-performance indoor golf club.Positioning: High performance, data-driven, coach-led — powered by TrackMan and Zen, delivered with premium service, clear outcomes, and a friendly vibe that never sacrifices credibility.9. Brand PersonalityWe must feel: precise, calm, confident, warm, credible, welcoming, current.We must never feel: snobbish, desperate, vague, corporate, condescending, stuffy, or salesy.Personality shorthand: the sharp performance coach who is also a great host — a data-driven expert with a genuine welcome.10. AudienceGolf enthusiasts of all levels and abilities.11. Tone of VoiceWe speak with clarity, confidence, and warmth. We sound like people who know golf and know the data, but who never talk down to anyone and never hide behind jargon.Clear — short sentences, plain language.Calm and confident — we know the craft, we state it plainly.Welcoming — we sound glad the person is here.Precise — we use the real number, not a vague impression.Honest — we say when something isn't right for the golfer.12. Messaging Framework"Real Golf, Indoors" — we are the real thing, and it's always available."One size doesn't fit all" — clubs, coaching and practice are tailored to the player."Data confirms" — TrackMan turns guesswork into certainty."Master your game, not someone else's" — coaching and fitting are personal.Enjoyable golf, warm and welcoming.13. The Experience — What Great Feels LikeEvery visit follows a rhythm that makes the golfer feel understood, confident, and clear on the next step.Welcome — greet within seconds, personally. The golfer is expected and glad to be here.Invite them back.14. How the Brand Guides Daily Behaviour (Staff Guide)14.1 With Every CustomerGreet every person within seconds. No one is ever left to look for a member of staff.Know their name and use it. Remember what they're working on. We are a club — that means we remember people.Ask before telling. Understand the golfer before you recommend anything.Show, don't just say. Use the data that's in front of us to make the point.Never over-promise results. Be honest about what we can and can't fix.Set the next step before they leave — rebook the session, book the fitting, schedule the follow-up. Every visit ends with momentum.Ask for the review, and thank them for trusting us — genuinely.14.2 In a FittingFit the data and the player's swing, never the margin or a brand we want to move.Change one variable at a time. No random chaos.Explain every change in plain language — why this head, why this shaft, what it improved.Always capture the baseline. No baseline, no credible comparison.Cover the build: loft, lie, length, swing weight, shaft, grip. The fitting and the build are one promise.No 'this one's just better.' Back it with the pattern, not the one best shot.14.3 In a Coaching sessionUse the data to coach, not to lecture. 'Golf coaching sessions shouldn't feel like school.'Give one clear, actionable thing per visit, backed by a number.Keep it encouraging — improvement is exciting, not intimidating.Link the coaching session to the wider picture: practice prescription, fitting conversation, membership. We serve the whole game.Rebook the next session before they leave.14.4 On the Floor &amp; FacilityOwn the Swing Shack Standards — clean bays, tidy mats, data screens visible and working.Handle enquiries fast. A slow response is a broken promise. Speed to lead matters every day.Keep the member/client details clean and the follow-ups done. A tidy CRM is part of the brand.Fix it or flag it. Never walk past a broken screen, a cluttered bay, or a tired ball.Log issues and repairs. No problem gets 'forgotten because nobody owned it.'14.5 With Each OtherBe direct and honest, kindly. Straight talk is a strength here — it keeps us fast and trustworthy.Support each other's roles: the seller, the provider, the technician are one team serving one golfer.Protect the standards for each other. The team that cleans up together keeps the club great.Ask for help early. No one quietly drowns — the business is better when we're honest about capacity.15. What The Brand Is NotNot a discount driving range.Not a place where beginners are looked down on.Not a sales floor pushing brands for margin.Not a venue where data is decoration instead of evidence.Not stuffy, not corporate, not precious, not 'sell at any cost.'16. Internal Brand Standard — The Litmus TestBefore any action, any message, any decision, any interaction, ask:Does this help the golfer get better?Is this honest and backed by data?Does this respect the player, no matter their level?Does this treat the golfer as an individual, not a transaction?Does this uphold the Swing Shack Standard?Would I be proud to say this is how Swing Shack behaves?If the answer is no, it doesn't belong. If you're unsure, this document is your guide — and when in doubt, choose warmth plus truth, always.17. Brand Mantra (Internal Compass)Real Golf. Real Data. Real Welcome.That is not our public tagline — it is the compass every member of the team carries. It keeps us honest, precise, and welcoming in every single interaction.18. Final SummarySwing Shack is South Africa's first true indoor golf club: a high-performance, data-driven, coach-led home for golfers who want to get better. We fit and coach to the real swing, we let the ball speak and the data confirm, and we make everyone feel welcome while we do it. Our promise is simple — the ball speaks, data confirms, feel seals the deal. That is the Swing Shack Standard, and it belongs in everything we do.
