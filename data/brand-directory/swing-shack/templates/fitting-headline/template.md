# Fitting headline (`ss-fitting-headline`)

Dark full-bleed venue photo with a thin translucent frame, a centred white benefit kicker, and a huge accent service lockup (iron or putter fitting). No logo, no CTA band.

## Platforms

- Instagram feed (`ig_post` 1080×1350)
- Instagram story (`ig_story` 1080×1920 — block re-topped below safe zone)
- Facebook uses the 4:5 feed canvas (`channel_canvas.facebook → ig_post`)

GBP is excluded.

## Copy budget

- **Kicker** (`caption_hook`): ≤ 24 chars at 59px uppercase — e.g. `HIT MORE GREENS WITH AN`
- **Lockup** (`service_lockup`): ≤ 15 chars one line at 121px — e.g. `PUTTER FITTING`
- Sidecar override: `compose_service_lockup`

## When to use

Tag calendar rows `post_type: fitting_headline`, or pin `template_id: ss-fitting-headline`. Untagged `ss-fitting` pillar posts keep the service frame.
