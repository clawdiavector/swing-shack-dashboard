# Swing Shack | Punctuation Rules

The voice is **data-driven coach, confident, educational, no-nonsense**. Punctuation
should be invisible: the reader should feel the data, not the punctuation that
delivered it. Below is how punctuation is used (and avoided) for Swing Shack.

## Standing rule (all copy)

- **No em dashes.** Use pipes `|`, commas `,`, full stops `.`, or colons `:`.
  Em dashes are banned across every brand.
- **One sentence per claim.** Swing Shack is one-fact-per-sentence. If you need
  a connector, use a period or a pipe.

## Sentence terminators

- **Period `.`** is the default. After a claim, after a CTA, after a fact.
- **Question mark `?`** is rare. Use only when the question is genuinely the hook
  (e.g. "What does the data say in 30 minutes?"). Rhetorical questions are
  not Swing Shack voice.
- **Exclamation `!`** is almost never. Save for member-milestones or "Booked!"
  confirmation lines in the Publish queue. Not in headlines or hooks.

## Conjunctions

- **Pipe `|`** is the primary separator for label:payoff pairs.
  ✅ `TrackMan truth | your slice has a cause | get fitted.`
- **Colon `:`** for setup:payoff when the setup is a noun phrase.
  ✅ `TrackMan result: 0.5° attack angle delta.`
- **Comma `,`** for clause joins inside a single sentence.
  ✅ `Off-centre strikes cost you distance, and the data proves it.`
- **Ampersand `&`** is allowed in product names ("Fitting & Coaching") only.
  Never as a conjunction in prose.

## Lists

- **Numbered lists** for ordered steps ("1. Book 2. Warm up 3. Hit 10").
- **Bullet lists** for unordered parallel claims.
- No nested bullets deeper than 2 levels.

## Quotation marks

- **Curly quotes preferred** (`"…"`, `'…'`). Straight quotes are tolerated
  for code/JSON paths but never in published copy.
- **Single quotes** for scare-quotes or air-quotes only.
  ✅ `We don't do 'mystery fittings': we measure.`

## Capitalisation

- **Sentence case** in headlines and CTAs. Title Case is not Swing Shack.
- **TrackMan** is the only brand word that must be capitalised exactly.
- **TrackMan bay** (lowercase bay), **fitting** (lowercase), **session** (lowercase).
- **Member** capitalised when referring to Swing Shack members. **member** lowercase
  when referring to a generic member of any club.

## Numbers

- **Numerals not words** for data: "0.5°", "30 minutes", "108 mph", "7 days".
- **Words for emphasis** ("five thousand balls" reads as emphasis, not stat).
- **Duration format** is "30-min session" with hyphen, "30-minute session" in prose.
- **No $ on prices** in headlines. Always spell out or omit. Use numbers
  only in the offer line.

## Parentheses

- Rare. Use only for clarity on a unit or a stat.
  ✅ `attack angle (within 0.5°)`
- Never for asides or jokes (that's Stick voice).

## Apostrophes

- **Curly apostrophe** `'`. Not straight `'`.
- **Possessives** form normally: "the coach's session", "the golfer's data".
- **Plurals** never take apostrophes: "1990s", "5 sessions", "10+ members".

## What to avoid

- ❌ Em dashes (banned everywhere).
- ❌ Trailing ellipses (`…`) in headlines. Mid-thought ellipses are fine in
  long-form captions only.
- ❌ Multiple exclamation marks (`!!`) never.
- ❌ All-caps for emphasis. Use italic or bold, not SHOUTING.
- ❌ Slash conjunctions (`and/or`, `fitting/coaching`): write "and" or pick one.
