# Swing Shack — Tone Rules

The voice is **data-driven coach, confident, educational, no-nonsense**. Below is how
that translates per tone for Swing Shack specifically.

## Educational
**Aim:** Teach one specific thing. No vague "improvement" claims.
- Open with the fact (the TrackMan number, the physics, the fitting truth).
- One sentence setup, one sentence payoff, one CTA.
- Never start with "Did you know…" — that is Stick voice.
- Reference what *will happen* in a session, not what the golfer "should" feel.

✅ "TrackMan shows attack angle within 0.5° in a 30-minute session."
✅ "Driver fittings isolate 4 variables — swing speed, attack angle, path, face — then match them."
❌ "Did you know your swing could be better?" (too generic, not Swing Shack voice)
❌ "Our coaches will help you improve your game." (vague, not Swing Shack)

## Confident
**Aim:** Make a bold, defensible claim that the data backs.
- Use TrackMan / fitting vocabulary to anchor credibility.
- Never hedge with "we believe" or "we think" — state the truth.
- One sentence. Then CTA.

✅ "Off-centre strikes cost you distance. The data proves it. Get fitted."
✅ "Your current driver isn't matched to your swing. The bay doesn't lie."
❌ "We think you might benefit from a fitting." (weak, not Swing Shack)
❌ "Our coaches are confident they can help." (false confidence)

## Funny (rare — Swing Shack is not a joke brand)
**Aim:** If humour is used, it must be the golfer's self-deprecation, not mockery.
- Never punch at the golfer's ability. The humour is shared, not directed.
- One line of levity, then back to data.

✅ "Your handicap will thank you. So will your wallet."
❌ "LOL slice again?" (not Swing Shack — this is Stick voice territory)

## Relatable
**Aim:** Acknowledge the golfer's real situation. Be specific.
- Name the situation ("3-footer to win the club champ"), then offer the data answer.
- Never be generic ("we all know golf is hard").

✅ "Winter golf in JHB: the simulator is the only honest feedback you'll get."
✅ "You've been fitted for shoes. Why not your driver?"
❌ "We know what it's like out there." (vague)

## Provocative (use sparingly)
**Aim:** Challenge one specific assumption.
- The challenge must be backed by something Swing Shack can demonstrate.
- Never just be edgy for its own sake.

✅ "Off-rack clubs. The cost-saving that costs you strokes."
❌ "Most golfers are wrong." (too generic)

## Banned across all tones
- Em dashes (use pipes | / commas / full stops / colons).
- Internal placeholders ("the team", "Memories", "Clawfix", "Retina", etc.).
- Fake TrackMan numbers / fitting outcomes / prices / screenshots.
- Generic "improve your game" claims — every line must be specific.
