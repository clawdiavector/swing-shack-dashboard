# Swing Shack — Do Say / Don't Say

## Do say (use these)
- TrackMan (capitalised, always)
- data, data-backed, data-driven
- fitting / custom fitting / driver fitting
- swing speed, attack angle, path, face, smash factor
- 30-minute session, 60-minute session (specific durations)
- simulator / indoor golf / TrackMan bay
- member / membership / members' bay
- coach / coaching (lowercase)
- swing, ball flight, dispersion, launch

## Say with care (use only when specific)
- "improve" — only when paired with a measurable (speed, distance, dispersion)
- "fix" — only for mechanical things (slice, hook, attack angle), not "fix your game"
- "guarantee" — never, unless verified per claim
- "best in JHB" / "the best" — never in published copy without Christelle sign-off
- "free" — only for verified free offerings (never invented)

## Don't say (banned or anti-pattern)
- ❌ "We'll help you reach your potential" — too vague
- ❌ "Take your game to the next level" — generic stock phrase
- ❌ "Unlock your potential" — banned across all brands
- ❌ "World-class" — banned unless verified
- ❌ "The ultimate golfing experience" — banned, too generic
- ❌ "Fun for the whole family" — not Swing Shack voice
- ❌ "Whether you're a beginner or a pro" — we are performance, not beginner
- ❌ "Look no further" — banned

## Numbers discipline
- ❌ "90% of golfers" / "30-metre accuracy" — CUT, needs data per verification-stats-driver-fitting-2026-06-22.md
- ✅ Specific TrackMan metrics when verified (attack angle in degrees, swing speed in mph, smash factor)
- ✅ Dates, session durations, fitting durations
- ✅ Member numbers only when verified (e.g. "200+ members")

## Em dash ban
- No em dashes anywhere. Use:
  - pipes: `headline | payoff | CTA`
  - commas: `headline, payoff, CTA`
  - full stops: separate sentences.
  - colons: `TrackMan truth: your slice has a cause.`
