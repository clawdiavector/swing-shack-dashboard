## Read this first: three findings that change what is safe to publish

1. **Performance numbers have been published that are not in any verified source.**
   Examples, all from the feed: "87% of golfers who get fit lower their handicap. That's
   fact, not opinion." (20 Nov 2025). "Fitted clubs can take up to 5 shots off your game
   guaranteed!" (23 Oct 2025). "20% more fairway accuracy after proper fitting" (3 Nov).
   "up to 25 meters" (20 Oct), "up to 19 metres" (10 Nov), "as many as 6 shots per round"
   (14 Nov). "From 165m slice to 265m straight", "Club speed: 142km/h to 158km/h",
   "Accuracy: +32%" under the words "Real South African results" (30 Mar 2026). "Pros average
   112 mph" and "264 meters" (1 Apr). The habit is older: "Golfers gain an average of 10
   meters in total distance with an optimally fit driver" (9 Feb 2024) and "87% of golfers
   reduce their handicap by at least 10% with properly fit clubs" (16 Feb 2024, then again
   on 20 Nov 2025). Swing Shack's own `knowledge.json` says: no "90% /
   30-metre" stats, no fake TrackMan numbers, no fake fitting outcomes, "any number not in
   this file is not allowed in copy". None of these numbers is in that file. Whether any of
   them came from real data is not recorded anywhere in the repo. They should be treated as
   unsubstantiated until someone shows the source, and "guaranteed" should come out of
   any future copy regardless. Advertising regulators expect performance claims to be
   substantiated, so this is more than a style point. The owners should look at these posts.
2. **The workbook's six recurring properties do not appear in a single caption, but the
   feed has series of its own that the workbook does not know about.** "What Does Your Swing
   Say?", "The 10-Ball Truth", "Shack Sessions", "The Truth About...", "Golf Is More Fun
   When...", "Before / After / Why": zero of 764 captions name any of them, and the platform
   line "Golf is more fun when it makes sense" appears in none. They may be on screen inside
   the videos, which the audit cannot see. What the captions do show: **Build of the Week**
   (24 captions, 28 Sep 2024 to 22 Jul 2026), **"Did you know"** (13, 23 Aug 2025 to 21 Jan
   2026), **Tip of the Week** (9, 2024), **"Myth vs Reality"** (6, Oct to Dec 2024), a
   weekly **"Big week at Swing Shack"** schedule post (10, Mar to Aug 2025), **course-of-the-week**
   "now on TrackMan" posts (11, 2025), **ambassador profiles** (12, Mar to Jun 2025), and
   **Shack Labs** (4, Sep to Oct 2025). Which set the owners want is decision 15.
3. **For about eleven weeks Swing Shack and Stick posted the same product copy with the
   name swapped.** 24 of Swing Shack's 200 captions share at least one full line with a
   Stick caption, 26 distinct lines in all, between 26 May and 5 August 2026. Five of the 26
   are calls to action ("DM us for price and details."), which are meant to repeat. Setting
   those aside, 20 captions share a line of actual copy, and the linter counts those. Titleist GTS,
   Scotty Cameron, Mileseey, Callaway Quantum, Sub 70, the Srixon bag and the ball-fitting
   campaign all ran as the same sentences on both feeds. Two sister brands with different
   promises cannot sound the same. The swap test (L6.2) now includes swapping the sister
   brand, section 17.

## 4. Strategy core

All `CONFIRMED`, from Bible v1 and the workbook.

- **Purpose.** To make golf more enjoyable by removing the guesswork. Replace uncertainty with clarity, in a space that feels human, not clinical. (Workbook B.) Bible v1 gives a fuller statement: help every golfer play their own best golf through data-led coaching, fitting treated as science, and an experience that makes getting better feel good.
- **Core belief.** Golf is more fun when it makes sense.
- **Brand promise.** The ball speaks. Data confirms. Feel seals the deal.
- **Public tagline.** Real golf, indoors.
- **Internal mantra.** Real golf. Real data. Real welcome. (Not public.)
- **Quote used on the website.** The swing may lie, but the ball tells the truth.
- **Positioning.** Johannesburg's indoor golf club for people who care about their game but still want to enjoy it. TrackMan-powered fitting, coaching and practice with a relaxed, human atmosphere, so improvement feels exciting, not like homework.
- **Personality.** Precise, calm, confident, warm, credible, welcoming, current. "The sharp performance coach who is also a great host." Never snobbish, desperate, vague, corporate, condescending, stuffy or salesy.
- **Ambition.** The authority on measurement-led golf improvement in South Africa.
- **Audience, primary.** Serious golfers in Johannesburg who want to improve, defined by mindset not handicap: club golfers of 5 to 20 handicap, players who have plateaued, returning golfers, the indoor-curious.
- **Audience, secondary.** Social and casual golfers: social play, TrackMan games, group bookings, a way to play whatever the weather.
- **They already know:** indoor golf exists, TrackMan is a launch monitor, fitting probably helps, golf is hard. Do not explain what a simulator is.
- **They worry that:** indoor golf is a toy, the data is intimidating, fitting will be an upsell, they are not good enough, the numbers will confirm how bad they are, indoor swings do not transfer.
- **Never make them feel:** intimidated, patronised, sold to, judged, confused by data with no conclusion, that indoor golf is "less than" real golf, that improvement is homework.
- **The litmus test (bible v1 section 16).** Does this help the golfer get better? Is it honest and backed by data? Does it respect the player at any level? Does it treat the golfer as an individual? Would I be proud to say this is how Swing Shack behaves? When in doubt: warmth plus truth.
- **Messaging priority (workbook E).** 1. Golf is more fun when it makes sense. 2. Know your numbers. 3. Fitting first, brand second. 4. Tech serves the player. 5. Real golf, indoors.

## 5. Voice

### 5.1 Character

- **Voice in five words:** warm, knowledgeable, witty, useful, confident (workbook D). Bible v1 adds: clear, calm, precise, honest, welcoming. `CONFIRMED`.
- **If Swing Shack were a person:** the coach who greets you by name, shows you the number, tells you the truth kindly, and has you booked in for next week before you leave.
- **Point of view:** data is there to make golf make sense, not to make golfers feel small.
- **We are, but not:**
  - precise, but not clinical: a number always comes with its meaning.
  - warm, but not gushing: welcome without exclamation marks.
  - witty, but not a comedian: dry lines, not skits or puns on every post.
  - confident, but not boastful: we state what we can show.
  - a club, not a clubhouse with a velvet rope.

### 5.2 Voice dials

| Dial | Setting | The rule that follows |
|---|---|---|
| Formality | 2 of 5 | Contractions are fine. No text-speak (`u`, `w us`, `lil`, `sesh`) |
| Humour | 2 of 5 | One dry beat at most. Never at the golfer's expense. Never on price |
| Boldness | 3 of 5 | State what the data shows. Never promise a result |
| Technicality | 3 of 5 | Max three numbers per post, each with its "so what" (workbook K). Lead with the golfer's outcome, not TrackMan |
| Warmth | 4 of 5 | Welcoming, never urgent. "Come and see what your swing says" beats "Limited time offer" |
| Energy | 2 of 5 | Calm. Exclamation marks are rare and genuine |
| Local flavour | 3 of 5 | Joburg is allowed to show: summer heat, highveld, Randburg. Not a braai ad |

### 5.3 Tone shifts by situation

| Situation | Tone | Length | Humour? | Opening line in the feed's best register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product arrival | Plain and specific | 15 to 30 words | A dry line if it earns it | `Titleist GTS is now available for fitting at Swing Shack.` |
| Fitting education | Patient, evidence-led | 20 to 40 words | Rare | `Your driver can feel fine.` |
| Coaching | Direct, encouraging, one idea | 15 to 30 words | Yes, dry | `A few small changes can save a lot of golf balls.` |
| Membership and packages | Clear, plain, complete | 25 to 45 words | None | State what you get, then the price, then how to join |
| Offer, competition, code | Understated, complete | 15 to 30 words | None | What, who, until when, how |
| Event or social | Warm and specific | 20 to 40 words | Light | Date, time and place first |
| New team member | Warm, short | 15 to 30 words | Light | `Dawid has joined the coaching team.` |
| Bad news (closure, delay, price change) | Plain, quick, no spin | 10 to 25 words | None | Say what happened and what happens next |
| Reply to a happy comment | Short and human | 5 to 20 words | Match theirs | Thank them, use their name if public |
| Reply to a complaint | Calm and specific, then private | 15 to 40 words | None | `Sorry about that. Can you DM us the details so we can sort it?` |
| Reply to a question we cannot answer | Honest | 10 to 25 words | None | `Good question. Let us check and come back to you today.` |

### 5.4 Humour, slang and sensitive ground

- **Humour that works (from the feed):** "The woods are lovely this time of year. Still, probably better to stay out of them." · "Off-the-rack is fine for groceries. For clubs, let's aim a little higher." · "Golf is easier when the club is built around your swing, not someone else's spec sheet." Understatement, specific, golfer-aware.
- **Humour that does not:** stacked puns ("At a loss 'fore' words"), 🤣 as punctuation, "Hit bombs", jokes about golfers' ability.
- **Slang allowed (workbook D):** `stripe it`, `flush it`, `dialled in`, `scoring zone`. **Never:** `crushing it`, `beast mode`, `lit`, `bro`, `fam`, `sesh`, `lil`, `u`.
- **Profanity:** none (workbook D).
- **Local language:** Joburg context is welcome and natural. No forced slang.
- **Never joke about:** a golfer's handicap, swing, body, age or money. Do not address people by gender ("Ladies, this one's for you", 17 Oct) unless the event is genuinely for one group, and then say so plainly.

## 10. Customer conversation

- **Who replies:** UNKNOWN. Name one person and a backup.

## 12. Photography and imagery

`CONFIRMED` from workbook G and N, plus Christelle's rules.

- **Look:** high contrast, deep blacks, slightly desaturated, cool shadows, neutral mids, slightly warm highlights. Never oversaturated.
- **Location:** the Swing Shack studio is the location: bays with the TrackMan screen glowing, the Zen Swing Stage, the workshop bench, signage on a dark wall. Never a golf course as the main setting.
- **People:** real golfers, coaches, fitters. The best single image is a golfer reacting to a TrackMan result.
