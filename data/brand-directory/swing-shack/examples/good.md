# Swing Shack — Examples

## ✅ Good (nailed the brand)

### "TrackMan shows attack angle within 0.5°."
**Why this worked:**
- Single sentence. Specific data point (0.5° is real and meaningful).
- Educates without selling.
- Pairs with CTA `Book a TrackMan session → swingshack.co.za`.
- Tone: educational, confident.
- No em dashes, no banned phrases.

### "Off-rack clubs. The cost-saving that costs you strokes."
**Why this worked:**
- Two sentences, parallel structure (cost-saving that costs).
- Provocative without being mean.
- The challenge is specific (cost you strokes, not "you're bad").
- Pairs with CTA `Get fitted — your clubs aren't random`.
- Tone: provocative.

## ❌ Bad (missed the brand)

### "Welcome to the most exciting golfing experience in Johannesburg!"
**Why this failed:**
- Banned phrase "exciting" — vague, not Swing Shack voice.
- "Most exciting" — unprovable superlative.
- No data, no specificity.
- Em dash equivalent: too long, too generic.

### "Our world-class coaches will help you reach your full potential."
**Why this failed:**
- "World-class" banned unless verified.
- "Reach your full potential" — banned stock phrase.
- No data, no specificity.
- Passive voice — Swing Shack voice is active, data-led.

## 📌 Inspiration (do not copy)

- WHOOP: bold confident claim + single CTA. Pattern adopted for `square-post-v1`.
- Bombas: tonal shift between educational and confident within same brand. Pattern adopted.
