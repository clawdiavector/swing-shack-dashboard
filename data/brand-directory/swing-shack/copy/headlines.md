- `Coach Mike has fitted 200+ drivers in JHB.` `[NEEDS DATA — verify count][tone:confident][pillar:coach_credibility][intent:credibility]`
- `Every TrackMan bay in JHB runs the same data. We use it.` `[tone:confident][pillar:trackman_intelligence][intent:credibility]`

## Pattern rules
- One sentence. No exceptions.
- Lead with the data, the truth, or the relatable situation. Never with "we".
- Never use em dashes. Pipes | / colons : / commas , / full stops only.
- Every headline pairs with exactly one CTA per `ctas.md`.

## Banned patterns
- ❌ "Unlock your potential"
- ❌ "Take your game to the next level"
- ❌ "Become the golfer you were meant to be"
- ❌ "Golf is hard, we make it easy"
- ❌ Any headline that doesn't name a specific thing.
