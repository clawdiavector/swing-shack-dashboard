# Swing Shack — CTAs

## Hard CTAs (action + URL)
1. `Book your session → swingshack.co.za`
2. `Book a TrackMan session → swingshack.co.za`
3. `Get fitted — your clubs aren't random`
4. `Try the 30-min data session → swingshack.co.za`
5. `Book a driver fitting → swingshack.co.za`
6. `Member bay waiting → swingshack.co.za`

## Soft CTAs (no URL, awareness-only)
1. `TrackMan doesn't lie.`
2. `Your clubs deserve better than you.`
3. `The bay doesn't lie.`
4. `Data over hope.`
5. `Fit first. Hit second.`

## CTA selection rules
- **Educational posts** → use a hard CTA (book a session).
- **Confident / bold claim posts** → use a soft CTA that echoes the claim.
- **Quote cards / coach credibility** → soft CTA or no CTA.
- **Promo / sale** → hard CTA, but only when there's a real offer.
- **Meme / funny** → soft CTA, never hard.

## Banned CTAs
- ❌ "Click here"
- ❌ "Learn more"
- ❌ "Sign up now"
- ❌ "Shop now"
- ❌ "Buy now" (Swing Shack doesn't sell clubs direct)
- ❌ Any CTA that mentions a price or discount unless verified.
