AudiencePrimary audience  Describe needs, behaviour, sophistication, objections and buying triggers.Serious golfers in Johannesburg who care about improvement — defined by mindset, not handicap. They: have a recurring miss or inconsistency they can't solve alone, are curious about TrackMan data and what their numbers actually mean, want professional fitting but don't want to be intimidated, value expertise over brand prestige, want golf to feel less frustrating and more enjoyable, are willing to invest in equipment that fits properly. Includes: club golfers (5-20 handicap), players who've plateaued, returning golfers with new equipment gaps, indoor golf curious, and people tired of guessing on the range.Buying triggers: a clear problem identified (consistent miss, distance gap, dispersion), an offer to 'find out what your swing says', pre-season readiness, new equipment curiosity after a bad round, member referral.________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________Secondary audiences  Only if materially different.Social / casual golfers — may not yet be deeply technical but want a fun way to play golf regardless of weather.
