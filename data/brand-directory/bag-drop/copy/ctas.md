# The Bag Drop | CTAs

One CTA per caption, and the "how" is part of it. From `BRAND-BIBLE.md` section 6 and
Part 4 `ctas_allowed`.

## Approved CTAs
1. `Shop the drop. Link in bio.` (THE DROP)
2. `View the product. Link in bio.` (one product)
3. `Shop putters. Link in bio.` (a category: also balls, wedges, grips and so on)
4. `Message us to order.` (one-off or limited items)
5. `Tell us what you are after.` (WANTED)
6. `Email hello@thebagdrop.co.za.` (returns and order questions)
7. `Visit the online store or DM us.` (the line the feed already uses)

## CTA selection rules
- **Product post** → 1 or 2, with the price in the caption.
- **Category post** → 3.
- **Single limited item** → 4, still with the price.
- **Sourcing ask** → 5.
- **Order or return question** → 6.

## Not yet
- `Join Drop Alerts.` Only once a sign-up exists.

## Banned CTAs
- `DM for price`, `Message us for current stock`, `Message us to check what's available`
  as the whole CTA: they hide the price.
- `Grab it before it's gone`, `Don't sleep on this one`.
- A pasted web address.
- Any CTA naming Swing Shack or Stick without Hermanus Kriel's approval.
