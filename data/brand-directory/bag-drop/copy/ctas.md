2. `Leave your bag at Swing Shack — play lighter`
3. `Member social every Thursday — join us`
4. `Book your drop → swingshack.co.za`

## Soft CTAs (no URL, awareness-only)
1. `You'll never go back.`
2. `Apologise never.`
3. `The bag that lives at Swing Shack.`
4. `Play lighter.`

## CTA selection rules
- **Relatable / warm** → soft CTA that lands the warmth.
- **Event (Thursday social)** → hard CTA, name the event.
- **Service (bag drop)** → hard CTA with URL.
- **Meme / funny** → soft CTA, never hard.

## Banned CTAs
- ❌ "Join the family" / "Join our community"
- ❌ "Limited spots" / urgency pressure
- ❌ "Learn more" / "Click here"
- ❌ Any CTA mentioning Stick or Swing Shack directly (Bag Drop voice stands alone)
- ❌ Any CTA that mentions a price or discount unless verified
