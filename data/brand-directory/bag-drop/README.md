`BRAND-BIBLE.md` is the source for everything here. Where this page and the bible
disagree, the bible wins.

## What this brand is
An online store for demo and pre-owned golf gear at `thebagdrop.co.za` (Shopify):
demo heads, pre-owned clubs, putters, shafts and the essentials (balls, gloves, grips,
towels). National shipping, condition grades, a 30-day return policy. Instagram
`@the_bagdrop_za`. Confirmed by Christelle on 2026-10-21.

Belief: `Good gear doesn't need to be new.`

## Who it is for
South African golfers who know what they want and do not want to pay new prices:
equipment nerds, club hoppers, golfers rebuilding a bag, fitters looking for demo heads.
They are online shoppers anywhere in the country, not visitors to a venue.

## What it is not
- Not a storage service and not part of a venue. There is no shop to visit.
- Not `bagdrop.co.za`, which is an unrelated luggage company.
- Not a bargain bin or a marketplace. Edited, not endless.
- Not hype. Price, condition and specs do the selling.

## Voice in five words
Sharp, honest, confident, curated, clever. Dry and plain. Bible section 5.
