# Bag Drop — Brand Directory One-Pager

## What this brand is
Community-first, member-energy voice. Fun, supportive, relatable. Refers to the Bag Drop
service, member social, shared golfer experience. Operates as a sub-brand of Swing Shack
(the service and bookings route through Swing Shack).

## Who it is for
Members and prospective members who want their golf life to feel social. The Thursday
member social crowd. People who care about the vibe as much as the data.

## What it is not
- Not a sales-y voice — Bag Drop is community, not promo.
- Not a separate business — service offerings route to Swing Shack.
- Not serious performance copy — Bag Drop is the warmth side.
- Not for "I just want to fix my slice" — that's Swing Shack voice.
