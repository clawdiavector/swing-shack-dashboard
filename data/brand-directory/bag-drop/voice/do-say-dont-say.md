# The Bag Drop | Do Say / Don't Say

From `BRAND-BIBLE.md` sections 3 and 6. The bible's Part 4 is what the caption linter
enforces.

## Do say
- `demo head`, `demo club`, `pre-owned`, `full club`, `head only`, `the drop`,
  `condition grade`
- `Good gear doesn't need to be new.`
- `Great golf gear, ready for another round.`
- `This just dropped.`
- `Actual item. Actual condition.`
- The price as `R6,500`
- The spec line as `Loft: 11°. Condition: B. Price: R5,000.`
- Grades as `A`, `B` or `New`, the store's own scale

## Say with care (max one per caption)
`premium`, `proper`, `clean`, `serious`, `sharp`, `smart`

## Don't say
- `second-hand`, `pre-loved`, `bargain`, `steal`, `cheap`, `as-is`
- `like new`, `mint condition`: show a grade and the wear instead
- `inspected`, `tested`: no recorded process backs them
- `DM for price`, `Message us for current stock`: never make the customer ask
- `Don't sleep on this one`, `Grab it before it's gone`, `Stock moves fast`
- `lethal`, `murdered-out`, `stealth mode`, `aggressively`
- `one careful owner`, `lady golfer`
- `Drop what you've got in the comments`
- `bagdrop.co.za`: the wrong company
- `SwingShack`, `Stick.paarl`

## Numbers discipline
- Every price, loft and grade matches the live listing on the day it posts.
- No follower counts, sales counts or "most popular" without a source.
- Offers only with terms written down (what, who, until when, how).
- Trade-ins, Drop Alerts and the fitting credit are not mentioned until the owners
  confirm them.

## Em dash ban
No em dashes or en dashes anywhere. No ellipsis.
