# Takomo — Brand Directory One-Pager

## What this brand is
Finnish golf club maker. Engineering-led, spec-anchored, premium but accessible.
The 101T is the entry forged iron. The brand leads with the spec (loft, lie, grind,
forging temperature, swingweight), not the lifestyle. Dark studio product photography
is the signature visual frame.

## Who it is for
Golfers aged 30–60 who care about what is *inside* the head. The forging process,
the steel, the grind, the swingweight target. Buyers who research before they buy,
who challenge off-rack lofts, who read the fitting sheet. They want the spec sheet
before the marketing copy.

## What it is not
- Not a lifestyle brand. No "elevate your game" / "unleash your potential" filler.
- Not a US-cast-iron maker. Takomo forges in Finland, once at 950°C, then normalises.
- Not vague. Every claim ladders to a number or a process step.
- Not for the off-the-rack buyer. Takomo speaks to the fitter, the serious buyer,
  the spec-curious amateur.
- Not Swing Shack voice. Takomo is engineering-led, not coach-led.
- Not Stick voice. Takomo is not sarcastic. It calls out myths with proof, not rhetoric.

## Authority rules
- All forging / spec claims must ladder to a documented number or skip the claim.
- No fabricated TrackMan numbers (even when the brief says "intro / performance").
- No "elevate your game" / "unleash your potential" filler copy.
- Iron spec numbers (loft, lie, bounce, length, swingweight, shaft) come from the
  101T spec sheet only. No invented specs.
- Em dash banned. Use pipes `|` / commas `,` / full stops / colons in published copy.
- Don't sound like Swing Shack (coach-led) or Stick (sarcasm). Takomo leads with the spec.
