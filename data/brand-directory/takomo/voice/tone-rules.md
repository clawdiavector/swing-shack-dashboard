# Takomo — Tone Rules

The voice is **engineering-led, spec-anchored, premium but accessible**. Takomo is a
Finnish golf club maker (the 101T is the entry forged iron). The audience is golfers
who care about what is *inside* the head — the forging process, the steel, the grind,
the swingweight target — not just the colour of the cosmetics. The voice respects that
audience by leading with the spec, not the lifestyle.

## Confident (primary)
**Aim:** Make a precise, defensible engineering claim. Never vague.
- Open with the spec (the loft, the grind, the forging temperature, the swingweight).
- One sentence setup, one sentence payoff, one CTA.
- Never start with "We believe" or "We think" — state the spec.
- Reference *what the iron does*, not what the golfer "should" feel.

✅ "27° of loft, 64° of lie, 7° of bounce — nothing extra, nothing missing."
✅ "The 101T is forged once at 950°C, then normalised. The grain is the spec."
❌ "We believe the 101T is a great club." (hedged, not Takomo)
❌ "Our irons will help you play better." (lifestyle, not Takomo)

## Educational (secondary)
**Aim:** Teach one spec-driven thing. No vague "feel" claims.
- Open with the spec, then the physics / process.
- Reference the forging process, the steel, the CG location, the offset.
- Stand behind the math — every claim should ladder to a number.

✅ "Forged 1020 carbon steel. The face is what the ball sees — it matters."
✅ "The 101T cavity is milled before forging. The mass sits where the strike lives."
❌ "Did you know forged clubs are better?" (too generic, not Takomo voice)
❌ "Our process is special." (self-aggrandising, not Takomo)

## Provocative (rare)
**Aim:** Call out a hidden cost. The cast-iron myth, the off-the-rack delta.
- One sentence. Then the spec. Then CTA.
- Stay on the engineering, not the marketing.

✅ "Cast faces are cheap. The strike is not."
✅ "Off-rack lofts are picked by the line, not the player."
❌ "Cast clubs are trash." (not Takomo — leave the rhetoric to Stick)

## Anti-pattern 1 — Don't sound like a corporate brochure
Takomo is a club maker, not a lifestyle brand. Avoid "elevate your game",
"unleash your potential", "experience the difference" — these are filler words.
Every claim should ladder to a number or a process step.

❌ "Elevate your golf game with the 101T."
✅ "The 101T sits at 7° of bounce. The turf tells you if that's right."

## Anti-pattern 2 — Don't fabricate numbers
Stand behind real specs or skip the claim. The 101T has documented loft, lie,
length, swingweight, shaft, grip — those are the anchors. Anything outside
those (COR, MOI, vibration frequency) is `[NEEDS DATA]` until verified.
