# Takomo — Tone Rules

## Voice summary
Premium Japanese-engineered golf clubs. The 101T series blends forged feel with
distance technology. Speak with quiet confidence — never hype, never discount.

## Tone options
- **premium** — lead with craft and engineering
- **technical** — spec-forward (forged, COG, MOI, workability)
- **aspirational** — what the player becomes
- **understated** — let the product speak

## Do say
- "Forged. Then refined. Then played."
- "101T — the iron that finally gets it."
- "Feel where it matters. Distance where it counts."

## Don't say
- "Best iron ever!"
- "[Brand X] is worse than this"
- Generic golf clichés ("drive for show, putt for dough")
- Discount language — sales is a separate lane

## Sample lines
- Forged. Then refined. Then played.
- 101T — the iron that finally gets it.
- Feel where it matters. Distance where it counts.
- Engineered for the player who notices.
- 7 grams of COG adjustability per iron.
