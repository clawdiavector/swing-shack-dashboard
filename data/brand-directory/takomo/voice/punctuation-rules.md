# Takomo | Punctuation Rules

The voice is **engineering-led, spec-anchored, premium but accessible**. Punctuation
is precise: the spec is the spec, the data is the data. Punctuation should
disappear behind the engineering. Below is how punctuation lands for Takomo.

## Standing rule (all copy)

- **No em dashes.** Use pipes `|`, commas `,`, full stops `.`, or colons `:`.
  Em dashes are banned across every brand.
- **Spec-first.** Numbers and units take the lead. Punctuation serves the spec,
  not the other way around.

## Sentence terminators

- **Period `.`** is the default. After a spec, after a verdict, after a CTA.
- **No question marks in headline copy.** Rhetorical questions are not Takomo voice.
  Save them for FAQ blocks and Reddit outreach.
- **Exclamation `!`** is never used in spec copy. Warm invites only in
  member/community content, and never with multiple marks.

## Spec formats

- **Numerals + units** with no space under SI convention: "32°", "8.5°", "112mm",
  "260g", "2.8mm". The unit is part of the spec, not a label.
- **Plus-minus `±`** for tolerances: "8.5°±0.5°", "260g±3g".
- **Hyphen `-`** for compound units and ranges: "30-min session", "8-10°",
  "260-280g".
- **Slash `/`** for ratios and ranges in technical specs: "5/4", "9.5/12".
- **Degree `°`** is part of the spec, never separated by a space from the number.

## Conjunctions

- **Comma `,`** for the engineering cadence:
  ✅ "Hardness, geometry, and weight distribution define the feel."
- **"and"** beats "&" everywhere except product names ("Takomo 5 & 7 Series").
- **Pipe `|`** for spec:verdict pairs:
  ✅ `8.5° face | 60° lie | 0.5° tolerance.`
- **Colon `:`** for the spec:payoff pattern:
  ✅ "Spec: 8.5° face, 60° lie, 0.5° tolerance."
- **Semi-colons `;`** are allowed in spec ranges when commas would crowd:
  ✅ "5 woods spanning 13° to 21°; 7 irons from 4-PW."

## Quotation marks

- **Curly quotes** preferred (`"…"`, `'…'`). Straight quotes never in published copy.
- **Double quotes** for direct engineer quotes in long-form content.
- **Avoid scare-quotes**. Takomo is literal, not ironic.

## Capitalisation

- **Sentence case** in headlines and CTAs.
- **Product names** keep their casing: "Takomo 5 Series", "Takomo 7 P irons".
- **Materials** are proper nouns: "Carbon Steel", "431 Stainless", "6061 Aluminum".
- **Loft / Lie / Length / Swing Weight** capitalised when used in spec tables only.
- **loft / lie / length** lowercase when used in prose.

## Numbers

- **Numerals always** for specs: "32°", "8.5°", "112mm", "260g".
- **Words for emphasis** ("a thousand fitting sessions", "ten thousand swings").
- **Decimals** to one decimal place for tolerances, two for finer specs.
- **No $ on prices** in headlines. Always spell out or omit ("from R-amount").

## Parentheses

- Allowed for unit clarification:
  ✅ "loft (degrees)"
- One parenthetical per spec line max. Don't nest.

## Apostrophes

- **Curly apostrophe** `'`. Not straight `'`.
- **Possessives** form normally: "the club's CG", "the iron's sole width".
- **Plurals** never take apostrophes: "1990s", "5 irons", "10 specs".

## What to avoid

- ❌ Em dashes (banned everywhere).
- ❌ Multiple exclamation marks (`!!`).
- ❌ Trailing ellipses (`…`) in spec copy.
- ❌ Rhetorical questions in headlines.
- ❌ ALL CAPS for emphasis. Italic or bold only.
- ❌ Sarcasm punctuation (combos like `!?`).
- ❌ Decorative punctuation. Every mark serves the spec.
