# Takomo — Do Say / Don't Say

## Do say (use these)
- forged, forging, 1020 carbon steel, 8620 carbon steel
- loft, lie, bounce, offset, swingweight, MOI, CG, COR
- grind, sole, top-line, blade, cavity, muscle-back
- shaft, flex, weight, kick-point, butt frequency
- grip, Golf Pride, Lamkin, Iomic, +1, +2, -1
- spec, specs, spec-driven, spec-anchored
- 101T, 101T-CB, 101T-MB, 201T, 101T Pro
- 950°C, normalised, normalised grain, grain flow
- CNC, milled, milled cavity, milled sole
- head only, head + shaft, full set
- custom build, custom fit, custom order
- "the strike", "the impact", "the ball-turf interaction"

## Say with care (only when specific)
- "forgiveness" — only with a number (e.g. "the 101T-CB is 7% more forgiving than the MB at off-centre strikes `[NEEDS DATA]`")
- "feel" — only with a spec (e.g. "soft feel from the 1020 carbon steel", not "incredible feel")
- "distance" — only with a launch-monitor number (never "longer", "huge distance")
- "tour" — only with a real tour-name (e.g. "DP World Tour", "PGA Tour")
- "premium" — only with a price anchor (e.g. "the 101T sits at R8,500 head-only")
- "best" / "the best" — never in published copy without Christelle sign-off

## Don't say (banned)
- ❌ "Revolutionary" / "game-changing" / "next-gen" — filler
- ❌ "Elevate your game" / "unleash your potential" — corporate brochure
- ❌ "Experience the difference" — empty
- ❌ "Hand-crafted" without saying by whom and where
- ❌ "Tour-level" unless naming the actual tour
- ❌ Any Takomo claim that mentions Swing Shack's bay, TrackMan, or the city of Johannesburg — Takomo is a separate brand and stands alone
- ❌ "Best in class" / "the best iron" — never without verified benchmark
- ❌ Imagined TrackMan numbers — every metric must be real or marked `[NEEDS DATA]`
- ❌ Made-up customer testimonials — no fake quotes
- ❌ Discount codes / pricing claims without verified input
- ❌ Em dashes (use colon `:` / pipe `|` / comma `,` instead) — standing rule

## Do reference (when the source is real)
- The 101T spec sheet (loft, lie, length, swingweight, shaft, grip) — these are the anchors
- The forging process (950°C, normalised, grain flow) — engineering credibility
- The cavity milling (CNC-milled before forging) — process detail
- The CG placement (low-CG, centred) — performance anchor
- The custom-build ladder (head only → head + shaft → full set) — order path
- Head-only R-price as the standard reference (verified value in takomo-101t brief)
