## CTA selection rules
- **Confident / spec-led** → hard CTA that ladders to the spec rail (build / spec / order).
- **Educational / process** → soft CTA that lands the spec, no URL.
- **Provocative** → soft CTA only. Never URL on a provocation.
- **Custom-build posts** → URL always points to the build rail (the order path), not the home page.
- **Spec / review posts** → URL always points to the spec rail, not the order path.

## Banned CTAs
- ❌ "Click here" / "Learn more" / "Sign up"
- ❌ "Shop now" / "Buy now" — Takomo is built-to-order, not "buy now"
- ❌ "Subscribe"
- ❌ Any CTA that mentions a price or discount unless verified per claim
- ❌ Any CTA that mentions Swing Shack, Bag Drop, or Stick by name — Takomo stands alone
- ❌ "Best in class" / "the best iron" — never in CTAs without verified benchmark
- ❌ Free trials without verification
- ❌ Em dashes in the CTA copy (use pipes `|` / colons `:` / commas instead)
- ❌ TrackMan numbers in the CTA copy unless the number is real and verified
