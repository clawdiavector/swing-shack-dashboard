# Takomo — CTAs

## Voice-fit CTAs (these work for Takomo's quiet premium tone)

- "Book your fitting →"
- "Feel the 101T →"
- "Try it on the range →"
- "See the spec →"
- "Talk to a fitter →"

## Avoid
- "BUY NOW"
- "% off" / discount language
- "Limited time"
- Hard-sell urgency

## Notes
CTAs are scoped to fitting-driven conversion, not retail. Swing Shack handles
the booking; Takomo content never has its own checkout.
