# Stick — CTAs

## Hard CTAs (action + URL)
1. `Get fitted. Your game deserves better.`
2. `Stop guessing. Get fitted.`
3. `Book a fitting before your next round → swingshack.co.za`

## Soft CTAs (no URL, awareness-only)
1. `Hope isn't a fitting strategy.`
2. `Your clubs deserve better than you.`
3. `TrackMan doesn't lie.`
4. `Not sure why you'd do it any other way.`

## CTA selection rules
- **Sarcastic / provocative** → soft CTA that echoes the headline. Hard CTA only on direct-response posts.
- **Meme / funny** → no CTA, or soft CTA that lands the joke.
- **Educational / confident** → hard CTA.
- **Promo / sale** → hard CTA, but only when there's a real offer.

## Banned CTAs
- ❌ "Click here" / "Learn more" / "Sign up"
- ❌ "Shop now" / "Buy now"
- ❌ "Subscribe"
- ❌ Any CTA that mentions a price or discount unless verified
- ❌ Any CTA mentioning Swing Shack or Bag Drop directly (Stick voice stands alone)
