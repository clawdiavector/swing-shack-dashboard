## Pattern rules
- One sentence (rarely two). No exceptions.
- Lead with the situation / the bad take. Then the truth. Soft CTA or no CTA.
- Irony / inversion over explanation.
- Never punch at handicap / age / gender / income.
- Em dashes banned.

## Banned patterns
- ❌ Mockery of any protected class
- ❌ "We're just being honest" — meta kills the joke
- ❌ Direct attacks on readers
- ❌ Cross-business mentions (Bag Drop, Swing Shack) without sign-off
