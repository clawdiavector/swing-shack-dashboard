# Stick | Punctuation Rules

The voice is **sarcastic, golf insider, calling out bad habits, meme-aware**. Stick
is the meme side of Swing Shack. Punctuation does a lot of comic timing work here:
the rhythm of the sentence is the punchline. Below is how punctuation lands (and
gets weaponised) for Stick specifically.

## Standing rule (all copy)

- **No em dashes.** Use pipes `|`, commas `,`, full stops `.`, or colons `:`.
  Em dashes are banned across every brand.
- **One sentence per beat.** Stick is set-up:knock-out. Two sentences max.
  Long sentences kill the joke.

## Sentence terminators

- **Period `.`** is the default. Stops the beat. The shorter the sentence, the
  bigger the laugh.
- **Question mark `?`** is a Stick staple. Use it to set up the irony:
  ✅ "Off-rack clubs. The budget option that costs you strokes. Change my mind."
- **Exclamation `!`** is rare. Save for ironic emphasis only:
  ✅ "Hope isn't a fitting strategy!" (the irony is incredulous, not excited)
- **Mixed punctuation** in one caption is fine if the contrast is the joke:
  ✅ "Promise? Get fitted. Tomorrow? Buy the off-rack set."

## The setup:payoff pattern

- **Period `.`** between setup and payoff. Not a comma. The full stop is the
  drumbeat that lets the punchline land.
  ✅ "Off-rack clubs. The budget option that costs you strokes."
  ❌ "Off-rack clubs, the budget option that costs you strokes."
- **Colon `:`** when the second half is a label or a verdict, not a punchline.
  ✅ "Range balls: the lie you keep telling yourself."
- **Question mark `?`** as the closing punctuation of a "change my mind" line.

## Lists

- **Staccato bullets** land harder than paragraph prose. Three short bullets
  beats a single long sentence.
  ✅ `- Hope. - Fitting. - Repeat.`
- Avoid numbered lists in Stick copy (they read as corporate, not meme).

## Quotation marks

- **Curly quotes** preferred (`"…"`, `'…'`). Straight quotes never in published copy.
- **Scare-quotes** are a Stick signature (but use sparingly, once per caption max).
  ✅ "Get 'fitted' at the range for free. Sure."
- **Air-quotes** are not required when you can just punch with the period.
  ✅ "Hire a coach" (no quotes needed; the period does the air-quotes).

## Capitalisation

- **Sentence case** is the default for headlines and CTAs.
- **ALL CAPS** is allowed for ironic emphasis on a single word per caption.
  ✅ "Hope isn't a fitting strategy."
  ✅ "Hope isn't a FITTING strategy."
  ❌ "HOPE ISN'T A FITTING STRATEGY." (one word max, not whole headline)
- **TrackMan** capitalised exactly, always.

## Numbers

- **Numerals** for stats and prices: "0.5°", "30 minutes", "R2,500".
- **Words for irony** ("thousands of golfers", "a hundred missed cuts").
- **Round numbers** carry the joke. "108 mph" sounds technical; "a hundred missed
  cuts" sounds like a meme.

## Parentheses

- Common for the side-thought or the meta-comment:
  ✅ "TrackMan says your attack angle is fine (your swing thoughts are not)."
- One parenthetical per caption max. Two reads as essay, not meme.
- Inside the parens, never prop up the punchline: the punchline is OUTSIDE.

## Apostrophes

- **Curly apostrophe** `'`. Not straight `'`.
- **Contractions** are Stick voice: "doesn't", "isn't", "won't".
  Stick code-switches to formal ("one does not") only for ironic effect.

## What to avoid

- ❌ Em dashes (banned everywhere).
- ❌ Multiple exclamation marks (`!!`). One per caption max.
- ❌ Ellipses as a thinking pause (`...`). Stick punches, it doesn't trail off.
- ❌ Quotation marks for emphasis. Use italic or bold, not "scare".
- ❌ Long sentences. Two sentences max per caption.
- ❌ Semi-colons. They are not in the Stick vocabulary.
