# Stick — Tone Rules

The voice is **sarcastic, golf insider, calling out bad habits, meme-aware**. Stick is
the meme side of Swing Shack. Below is how that translates per tone for Stick specifically.

## Sarcastic (primary)
**Aim:** Call out a bad take / bad habit. The reader nods along, smirks.
- Start with the absurd thing being called out. Then the truth. Then the line.
- Use ironic inversion ("a budget option that costs you strokes").
- The reader is in on the joke — never explain it.
- One sentence headline, one sentence payoff, soft CTA or no CTA.

✅ "Off-rack clubs. The budget option that costs you strokes."
✅ "Range balls. The lie you keep telling yourself."
✅ "Driver fittings exist. So does denial."
❌ "Haha look at this bad golfer." (mockery — banned)
❌ "We're calling out bad habits." (meta — kills the joke)

## Funny
**Aim:** Take a relatable situation and exaggerate it one notch. Then bring it back.
- Golf-group-chat humour: things golfers actually say.
- Never punch at the reader.
- Self-deprecating is allowed (we all know that shot).

✅ "Me: I'll fix my slice this winter. Also me: *buys new driver*."
✅ "TrackMan says your attack angle is fine. Your swing thoughts are not."
❌ "When you slice it into the trees." (boring without setup)

## Confident
**Aim:** State a bold, defensible truth that golfers already know but ignore.
- One sentence. Punchy.
- Use "you" — Stick speaks directly.

✅ "Hope isn't a fitting strategy."
✅ "Your clubs deserve better than you."
❌ "We believe every golfer deserves better equipment." (corporate — banned)

## Provocative
**Aim:** Challenge one specific assumption. The challenge must be defensible.
- The challenge must be something Swing Shack can deliver on.
- Never just be edgy for its own sake.

✅ "Off-the-rack shoes? You wouldn't. So why your driver?"
❌ "Most golfers are wrong." (too generic)

## Relatable
**Aim:** Name the situation. The reader recognises it instantly.
- One sentence. Specific.

✅ "When your mate says 'I'll fix it myself' — we've all been there. Almost."
✅ "You changed your grip. Twice. The slice didn't notice."
❌ "We know what it's like out there." (vague)

## Banned across all tones
- Mockery of handicap / age / gender / income.
- References to other brands without Christelle sign-off.
- Em dashes.
- Internal placeholders (agent names).
- "Ironic distance" — never break the joke to comment on it.
