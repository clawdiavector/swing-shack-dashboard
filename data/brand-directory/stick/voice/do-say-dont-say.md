# Stick — Do Say / Don't Say

## Do say (use these)
- off-rack clubs, off-the-rack, range balls, range myths
- slice, hook, top, chunk, sky
- fitting, driver fitting, club fitting, TrackMan
- hope, denial, excuses
- budget option, cost-saving (ironic inversion)
- "we" sparingly, "you" frequently

## Say with care
- "your slice" — only with empathy, never mockery
- "your handicap" — never as punchline, only as relatable framing

## Don't say (banned)
- ❌ "join the club" / "become a member" — Stick does not sell membership
- ❌ "we are passionate about golf"
- ❌ "your journey"
- ❌ "unlock your potential"
- ❌ "world-class"
- ❌ "bespoke"
- ❌ "game changer"
- ❌ "next level"
- ❌ "the perfect driver"
- ❌ "LOL" / "LMAO" — text-speak is not Stick voice
- ❌ "data shows" / "research shows" / vague study claims — say it plainly or omit
- ❌ Emojis as filler (one emoji max per post)
- ❌ "We'd never" / "We'd totally" — too informal
- ❌ "Bro" / "Fam" — Stick is not bro-voice
- ❌ Any reference to other brands (Bag Drop, Swing Shack) unless Christelle approves

## Numbers discipline
- ❌ No fake TrackMan numbers
- ❌ No fake fitting outcomes
- ❌ Distance claims like "15 metres" without a verified source
- ✅ "200+ drivers fitted" only when verified
- ✅ Dates, session durations, fitting durations

## Em dash ban
- No em dashes anywhere. Use pipes | / commas / full stops / colons.
