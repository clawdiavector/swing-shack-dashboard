STICKFull Brand BibleDraft v31. Brand FoundationWhat STICK isSTICK is a modern golf performance environment for players who want to get better without buying into the old, stuffy culture that has traditionally surrounded the game.It combines:coachingfittingequipmentapparelmodern golf cultureinto one brand experience built around improvement.This is not a traditional golf shop.It is not a country-club extension.It is not a simulator bar pretending to be serious.STICK is a place where attitude and performance meet on equal terms.The original brand material already points toward a brand built on technical precision, a modern rebellious edge, and a refusal to hide behind golf clichés. It explicitly frames the brand as moving away from “the traditional vibe” toward a “high-performance aesthetic,” and describes the tone as direct, confident, focused, technical, and minimalist. 2. Brand EssenceBetter golf for a new breed of golfer.That is the heart of the brand. It has nothing to do with age, gender, social status.STICK exists to prove that golfers do not need to conform to old golf culture to take improvement seriously.You can care deeply about the game, the gear, the numbers, the look, the feel, and the culture without becoming part of the stale establishment that has historically acted like it owns golf.3. Brand PurposeTo open the door to better golf for people who want to improve, while rejecting the outdated idea that serious golf must come wrapped in stuffy culture, legacy gatekeeping, and expensive nonsense.4. Brand MissionTo help golfers get better through:smart coachingplayer-first fittingproperly matched equipmentmodern product curationa welcoming, high-performance environmentSTICK matches equipment to the player, not the player to the equipment.That principle sits underneath everything.5. Brand VisionTo become the benchmark for modern golf culture and performance retail in South Africa, and ultimately a reference point for what a contemporary golf brand can be:credibleculturally relevanttechnically sharpaccessibledistinctive6. Brand PromiseBetter Begins Here.Every part of the STICK experience must make that line true.A golfer must leave feeling that improvement has genuinely begun, whether through:a lessona fittingbetter equipment choicesbetter product understandinga stronger connection to the game7. Brand ValuesThese are the non-negotiables.7.1 AttitudeSTICK has edge.It does not ask traditional golf culture for approval. It does not mimic old golf retail etiquette. It does not flatten its personality to seem respectable.The attitude is not decoration. It is part of the brand’s reason for existing.7.2 PrecisionSTICK takes golf seriously.The game is technical. Equipment matters. Data matters. Setup matters. Details matter.This seriousness is not old-fashioned or formal. It is disciplined.7.3 AccessSTICK believes serious golf must be more open.Players must be able to engage with quality product, great advice, and meaningful improvement across different budgets and backgrounds.7.4 HonestySTICK speaks plainly.No hype. No fake luxury theatre. No pretending that expensive automatically means better.7.5 ProgressThe brand is obsessed with improvement.Golfers improve. The game evolves. Brands evolve. Culture evolves.STICK is built for forward motion.8. STICK does not.9.2 The brand is player-firstSTICK matches product to player.
