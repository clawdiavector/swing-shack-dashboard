## 4. Strategy core

All `CONFIRMED`, from Brand Bible v3 and the Strategy 2026 to 2029. Condensed for the desk.

- **Purpose.** To open the door to better golf for people who want to improve, while rejecting the idea that serious golf must come wrapped in stuffy culture, legacy gatekeeping and expensive nonsense.
- **Core belief.** Everything has to earn its place.
- **Public promise.** Better begins here.
- **Fitting rule.** Fit first. Buy second. (Matches the equipment to the player, not the player to the equipment.)
- **Behaviour rule.** Respect the player. Never use expertise to make the golfer feel stupid.
- **Positioning.** The golf shop with a standard. Modern golfers come to get better, and everything has to earn its place.
- **Archetype.** The Smart Rebel: Rebel plus Sage. "A brand with edge and brains. Not a punk brand. Not an engineering robot."
- **Ambition.** Be the golf shop South African golfers trust to tell them what is actually worth it.
- **Audience, primary.** The "Any Golfer": defined by mindset, not age or handicap. Wants to improve, curious about equipment, values expertise over prestige, open to modern brands, not interested in snobbery.
- **Audience, secondary.** The culturally curious golfer: drawn to modern style and a less intimidating way in, often through Psycho Bunny or indoor golf.
- **They already know:** improvement is possible, equipment matters, fitters and coaches exist. Do not explain what a driver or a fitting is.
- **They worry about:** wasting money, being "fitted wrong" or not knowing enough, being sold something for margin, price shock, too much data.
- **Never make them feel:** patronised, sold to, confused, cheap, generic, intimidated.
- **Enemy.** The old golf culture that equates seriousness with stuffiness, formality and overpriced convention.
- **Pillars (starting shares, not quotas).** Product and brands 40%. Fitting and proof 25%. Workshop and service 20%. Culture, style and people 15%.
- **Stays out of.** Mockery of handicap, age, gender or income. Fake luxury. Country-club heritage. Talking as though only Paarl golfers can buy or be fitted ("proudly Paarl", not "only Paarl").

## 5. Voice

### 5.1 Character

- **Voice in five words:** sharp, confident, direct, smart, rebellious. `CONFIRMED` (workbook D, bible 16).
- **How that sounds in the feed:** calm, plain and dry. The rebellion is in what Stick refuses to say, not in volume.
- **If Stick were a person:** the fitter who has seen every excuse, tells you the truth without making you feel small, and does not need to raise their voice.
- **Point of view:** better golf does not require buying into old golf culture, or buying the most expensive thing.
- **We are, but not:**
  - confident, but not cocky: we state, we do not boast.
  - dry, but not sarcastic: the joke is shared, never aimed.
  - technical, but not a lab: a number always comes with its conclusion.
  - local, but not parochial: proudly Paarl, open to the whole Western Cape.
  - edgy, but not hostile: "confidence, not resentment" (bible 16).

### 5.2 Voice dials

| Dial | Setting | The rule that follows |
|---|---|---|
| Formality | 2 of 5 | Contractions are fine. No slang that dates quickly. No "Dear golfer" language either |
| Humour | 2 of 5 | One dry beat per caption at most. Understatement over punchline. Skip it when the subject is a price or a booking |
| Boldness | 4 of 5 | State the view. "Guessing is expensive golf." No hedging |
| Technicality | 3 of 5 | Numbers only with a conclusion beside them. Lead with the golfer's outcome, never with TrackMan |
| Warmth | 3 of 5 | "Respect the player" is the warmth. Invite, never lecture |
| Energy | 2 of 5 | Calm. No exclamation marks, no caps for volume |
| Local flavour | 2 of 5 | Name the place (Paarl, Winelands, Stellenbosch). No braai-ad voice |

### 5.3 Tone shifts by situation

| Situation | Tone | Length | Humour? | Opening line, in the real feed's register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product arrival | Plain and specific | 15 to 30 words | A dry line if the product earns it | `Titleist T250 iron set available at Stick.` |
| Why it's here | Editorial, opinionated, honest about trade-offs | 30 to 45 words | Light | `Why it's here: Sausage Putters.` |
| Fit first and proof | Patient, clear, evidence-led | 30 to 50 words | None | `Bring your current clubs.` |
| Coaching tip | Direct, one useful thing | 20 to 40 words | Rare | `A lesson should leave you knowing what to do next.` |
| Offer | Understated, access not clearance | 15 to 25 words | None | State the offer, who, until when, how to claim |
| Event or opening | Warm and specific | 20 to 40 words | Light | Date, time, place first |
| Style and apparel | Playful, a little arch | 15 to 30 words | Yes, this is where it lives | `The Bunny has landed.` |
| Bad news (delay, stock out) | Plain, quick, no spin | 10 to 25 words | None | Say what happened and what happens next |
| Reply to a happy comment | Short and human | 5 to 20 words | Match theirs | Thank them by name only if they are public |
| Reply to a complaint | Calm, specific, private | 15 to 40 words | None | `Sorry about that. Can you DM us the details?` |
| Reply to a question we cannot answer | Honest | 10 to 25 words | None | `Good question. Let us check and come back to you today.` |

### 5.4 Humour, slang and sensitive ground

- **Humour that works (all from the feed):** "Try not to leave with something. No promises." · "No one hovering like a security guard at a jewellery counter." · "Style with a pulse." · "Cute logo. Dangerous charm." Understatement, specific images, the golfer's own habits.
- **Never:** mock handicap, age, gender, income or ability. No sarcasm at a customer. No "tag someone who...". No humour on price, injury, complaints or bad news.
- **Slang allowed:** mate (used 5 times), edge, drop, setup. **Avoid:** `lit`, `fire`, `bro`, `fam`, `hit bombs`.
- **Profanity:** none. The 12 September caption says "bloody good space". Workbook D says none. Rewrite it.
- **Local words:** place names. "Mate" sparingly. No forced Afrikaans or township slang.
- **Never joke about:** a customer's swing or body, money trouble, injuries, anyone who has not agreed to be in the joke.

## 10. Customer conversation

- **Who replies:** UNKNOWN. Name one person and one backup.
- **Speed:** within a working day for DMs and comments. `DRAFT`.
- **Comments:** thank briefly, answer the question, move any price or booking detail to DM. Never argue in public.
- **DMs:** first reply names what they asked about and what happens next. "Hi [name], thanks for asking about the [product]. It is [in stock / can be ordered]. Want to book a fitting or come in?"
- **Complaints:** acknowledge, apologise for the experience without admitting fault in public, take it private, fix it, never delete a fair complaint.
- **Reviews:** thank, be specific, never defensive. Same voice as the feed.
- **Escalate to an owner:** pricing disputes, injury, legal threats, press, anything about a named person, any complaint about a fitting outcome.
- **Sensitive moments:** pause scheduled posts, hold a short plain statement, owner approves.
- Reply examples are in section 5.3.

## 12. Photography and imagery

`CONFIRMED` from workbook G, plus Christelle's rules.

- **Look:** performance minimalism with attitude. High contrast, deep blacks, product as hero, slightly desaturated, never oversaturated.
- **Location:** the Stick studio is the location. Dark matte surfaces, wood, stone, the fitting bay, the workshop bench, the shaft wall. Never a golf course as the primary setting.
- **People:** real golfers, fitters and coaches. Not models. Natural expressions. Hands at work in the workshop. A golfer reacting to a TrackMan result.
- **Real over generated:** never render a fake bay, a fake product or a fake venue. Real location and product photos exist for Stick. (Christelle, 2026-10-12.)
- **Grade:** blacks at `#121212` or deeper. Skin tones natural. Product colours exact. Amber and teal in overlays only, never on the product.
- **Rejected looks:** course backgrounds, faux luxury (marble, gold, crystal), pro-shop clutter, leather, mahogany, tartan, brass, over-posed stock models, product with the brand name larger than the product or the reason.
- **Image generation:** allowed only for genuinely new looks that photography cannot supply, never for product, venue or people. The workbook's master negative prompt (section R) applies.
