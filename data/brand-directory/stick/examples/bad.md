# Stick | Examples That Missed

## ❌ Bad (missed the brand)

### "We're just being honest: most golfers need a fitting."
**Why this failed:**
- "We're just being honest", meta kills the joke (banned pattern, tone-rules.md).
- "Most golfers", vague, unprovable.
- Reads as a soft corporate announcement, not a Stick post.
- No specificity, no inversion.

### "Haha look at this bad golfer! 😂"
**Why this failed:**
- Mockery of skill level, banned across all Stick tones.
- Emoji as filler, Stick allows one emoji max, not as a punchline prop.
- Punched at the reader instead of with the reader.
- No pairing with a specific bad habit or insight.

### "Experience the future of club fitting at Swing Shack!"
**Why this failed:**
- "Experience the future", vague, corporate, banned.
- "Swing Shack" cross-business mention without sign-off (do-say-dont-say.md bans it on Stick).
- Exclamation mark + superlative = announcement voice, not Stick voice.
- No irony, no inversion, no truth.

### "Golf is hard. Don't give up!"
**Why this failed:**
- Generic encouragement, no specificity.
- "Don't give up", negative framing, banned (Stick is positive inversion, not pep talk).
- No pairing with a real Stick moment (slice, range balls, off-rack clubs).
- Reads as a feel-good post, not a Stick post.

## 🚫 Banned patterns to keep catching

- "We're just being honest" / "Let's be real", meta kills the joke.
- "LOL" / "LMAO" / "Fam" / "Bro", text-speak, banned.
- Mockery of handicap, age, gender, income, banned.
- Vague superlatives: "most", "best", "world-class", "experience", banned.
- Cross-business mentions (Bag Drop, Swing Shack) without Christelle sign-off.
- Em dashes.
- Exclamation marks in headlines, Stick uses period or no punctuation.
