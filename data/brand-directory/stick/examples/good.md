# Stick | Examples

## ✅ Good (nailed the brand)

### "Off-rack clubs. The budget option that costs you strokes."
**Why this worked:**
- Two sentences, parallel structure (budget option that costs you).
- Ironic inversion: the cost-saving is the cost.
- Pairs with the `stick-callout` archetype on a single black panel.
- Tone: sarcastic, confident.
- No em dashes, no banned phrases.

### "Range balls. The lie you keep telling yourself."
**Why this worked:**
- Names the specific bad habit, then the truth.
- "Lie" lands without insulting the reader.
- Pairs with CTA `Get fitted. The lie ends on day one.`
- Tone: sarcastic, provocative.
- Reader is in on the joke, no explanation needed.

### "Me: I'll fix my slice this winter. Also me: *buys new driver*."
**Why this worked:**
- Two-line thread, classic group-chat humour.
- Self-deprecating, never punches at the reader.
- Italics render as the second beat, third-person framing.
- Tone: funny, relatable.
- Pairs with the `stick-thread-card` archetype.

### "Hope isn't a fitting strategy."
**Why this worked:**
- One sentence, seven words, defensible truth.
- Uses "you", Stick speaks directly.
- Pairs with `stick-callout` on a black panel, no CTA.
- Tone: confident.

### "You changed your grip. Twice. The slice didn't notice."
**Why this worked:**
- Three beats: action, escalation, truth.
- Empathetic without being soft.
- Reader recognises the moment, no sell.
- Tone: provocative, relatable.
- Pairs with `stick-tweet-card` archetype.

## 📌 Inspiration (do not copy)

- Golf Twitter accounts that lean in-joke over explanation. Pattern adopted for the `sarcastic` tone and the `stick-callout` archetype.
- Reddit r/golf top-of-week posts: specific moment, one-line payoff, no ask. Pattern adopted for the `relatable` tone.
- TrackMan session recap captions that lead with the data point, not the swing tip. Pattern adopted for the `confident` tone.
