Stick Golf · Brand &amp; Commercial Strategy · 2026–2029Everything has to earn its place.    A long-term strategy for a business that is part fitting studio, part workshop, part modern golf retailer and part editor.    Stick should not become another golf shop with more stock. It should become the filter golfers trust between the noise of the golf industry and what is actually worth buying.  Executive thesisStick is a standard. That idea already exists in the brand and it is the strategy.      Clubs have to earn their place through performance and fit. Shafts through compatibility. Balls through the way they perform for the player.      Bags through design and usefulness. Apparel through quality and attitude. Brands through relevance.      Stick's advantage is not having more things. It is having a point of view on why something deserves to be there. What the history saysProduct demand is not a side story.Historical Stick reports repeatedly show product-led content and ads generating useful behaviour. Bag campaigns produced inexpensive messaging conversations, Vessel and putter campaigns created direct enquiries, and fitting-led product posts repeatedly appeared among visible top content.Education improves the retail proposition.Fit Fact has been one of the strongest recurring lead-generation ideas. The historical 15 Jul–13 Aug view produced 44 leads at R20.10 each; the later 11–24 Aug window produced 24 lead-form results at R32.06 each.Paid social buys scale; search brings intent and depth.In the latest acquisition view, Paid Social contributed 647 users with 6 seconds average engagement, while Organic Search contributed 158 users with 1 minute 23 seconds. The implication is not to reduce paid media. It is to let paid distribution create demand while product, fitting and service content gives people something worth investigating.Specific product pages can hold attention.Takomo content has shown strong depth relative to its small audience. The Takomo landing page recorded 2m08s average engagement in a July–August view, and Search Console later showed 1,079 impressions and 39 clicks over three months.Local intent is commercially useful.Google Business Profile generated 1,161 interactions, 108 calls and 553 website clicks across the supplied March–August view. Stick's market starts in Paarl, but the customer footprint is wider: earlier GA4 location data showed Cape Town materially larger than Paarl.The recurring commercial pattern:the best Stick marketing does not force a choice between products and expertise. The market: where Stick can be differentFITTING COMPETITIONTechnology is not ownableWestern Cape fitters compete on launch monitors, shaft breadth, fitting expertise and workshop capability. Stick cannot win merely by saying “TrackMan” or “large shaft matrix”.RETAIL COMPETITIONProducts are available elsewhereGolf retailers can carry overlapping brands, and direct-to-consumer brands increasingly make themselves easy to buy. Availability alone does not create a brand.STICK'S SPACEEditorial taste + technical proofStick can combine fitter-level judgement, workshop credibility and a modern product point of view in one place. That combination is the advantage.Geographic position:Paarl is the home; the Western Cape is the market.      Historical GA4 location views have repeatedly shown a large Cape Town audience alongside Paarl, Stellenbosch and the Winelands.      The content should feel proudly Paarl without talking as though only Paarl golfers can buy, enquire or get fitted.5. Repetition: when is there enough evidence to make a decision?INTERNAL FAMILIARITY IS NOT MARKET SATURATION.      The team sees every caption, every edit and every post. The audience does not.      A new follower arrives today. A golfer sees one Story but misses the Reel. Someone searches three weeks later.      The temptation to say “we've done that already” is one of the easiest ways to kill memory before it is built.    One post is an exposure. What Stick should stop doingStop treating one product post as product marketing. A priority line needs a campaign and repetition.Stop changing the message because the internal team has seen it too often. Change the execution before changing the strategic idea.Stop making products look like catalogue filler. Every featured product needs a reason, a golfer and a next action.Stop using TrackMan as proof for things that do not require TrackMan. Technical credibility should not flatten the apparel and lifestyle side of the brand.Stop separating retail and fitting. Fit-led products and product-led fitting are two sides of the same commercial model.Stop thinking Paarl is the entire audience. The data repeatedly shows Cape Town and the broader Western Cape matter.Stop letting product campaigns disappear after launch week. New audiences arrive continuously and light category buyers need reminders.The one-page strategyWho is Stick? A modern golf shop with a standard.What is the ambition? Be the golf shop South African golfers trust to tell them what is actually worth it.What is the belief? Everything has to earn its place.What is the public promise? Better begins here.What is the fitting rule? Fit first. Buy second.What is the distinctive product property? Why It's Here.What is the behaviour rule? Respect the player.What makes the business different? Product taste + fitting proof + workshop capability + honest explanation.How long before we judge? Not after one or two posts: priority products get a real 4–6 week campaign; brand platforms get a quarter.What needs attention now? Sustain Psycho Bunny, keep product content permanently in rotation, and turn the Stick Standard into a visible system across store, social, search and paid media.
