teal band, wordmark + tagline footer. No hero photo.

  pixels (`#083E56` → `#063442`; vertical slope ≈ 0, so direction = horizontal)
- Headline: Montserrat Black (`display`), 3 lines, left aligned, ink from

## Bold CTA runs

`emphasis_font_role: cta_emphasis`. Compose fields stay plain text
(`"Book your free swing assessment"`); emphasis is applied per word at render.
