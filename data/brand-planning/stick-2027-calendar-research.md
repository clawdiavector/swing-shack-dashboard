## A. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Stick operates in **three integrated pillars — Coaching, Fitting, Retail** — under one big idea: **The Stick Standard.** This is not a content calendar. It is an **annual strategic pin-board**: the fixed moments the year hands us, mapped against what Stick should achieve commercially in each quarter.

### The biggest 2027 calendar opportunities:

1. **South African summer tourism swing (Oct 2026 → Mar 2027)** — Capetonian/Winelands leisure traffic into Paarl peaks. Retail (especially apparel, balls, accessories, giftable items) + walk-in fitting appointments are the highest-value moments of the year. **A-PIN.**

2. **Investec South African Open (Dec/Jan traditional slot)** — flagship Sunshine Tour + DP World Tour co-sanctioned event. National broadcaster, Western Cape region host in many years. **A-PIN.**

3. **Masters Week (Apr 8–11, 2027)** — global golf attention. Fitting appointments for "I want to play like them" + retail momentum on driver/putter weeks. **A-PIN.**

4. **Ryder Cup (Sep 17–19, 2027, Adare Manor, Ireland)** — Team event, even-year cycle, biggest moment in biennial golf. Massive content window + fitting spike for the SA market. **A-PIN.**

5. **The Open Championship (Jul 15–18, 2027)** — links golf month. Retailing of softer-feel gear (wedges, balls, gloves) + fitting for low-flight iron shots. **A-PIN.**

6. **Festive Season retail arc (Nov → Jan)** — vouchers, giftable apparel, end-of-year tax-reset fit appointments, January "New Year New Bag" push. **A-PIN.**

7. **Heritage Day (Sep 24, 2027)** — Friday long weekend. Local golf traffic + BBQ + community sport events. Coachable junior moments, parent-child fitting. **B-PIN.**

8. **Mother's Day (May 9, 2027)** — giftable apparel + vouchers + "buy her a lesson" angle. **B-PIN.**

9. **Father's Day (Jun 20, 2027)** — biggest gift-purchase moment of the year for SA golf. Fitting vouchers, premium club drops, apparel bundles. **A-PIN.**

10. **Back-to-school (Jan 2027)** — junior coaching, beginner-friendly content, parent-child lesson bundles, holiday retail push for junior apparel + entry-level sets. **B-PIN.**

### Key strategic principles applied:
- All three pillars (coaching, fitting, retail) are **always active**. No month is "retail only" or "fitting only".
- The big idea threads through everything but **does not need to appear in every caption.** It is the logic behind the lanes, not a literal line under every product.
- **Women in golf** are not a side note. SA Women's Open, women's majors, and women's coaching content run as a parallel thread through the year.
- **Local Winelands relevance** — Paarl/Stellenbosch/Franschhoek events are B-PINs and Stories/Carousel hooks, not full campaigns, with the exception of Cape Epic which can be a full A-PIN because of Western Cape golf traffic.

## B. NON-NEGOTIABLES

### Overall Stick Strategy (5)
1. **The big idea is the consistency, not the caption.** Stick does not restate "Better Begins Here" on every post. The big idea is visible in *what* is posted (curated, edited, earned) and *how* (sharp, confident, direct, smart, rebellious). Captions stay clean.
2. **All three pillars run every week, every month, every quarter.** Retail, fitting and coaching are *simultaneously* active. No exceptions for "slow months."
3. **Every product on the rack has a reason to be there.** Stick's editorial voice (Why It's Here / Fit First / Built At Stick / Ask Stick / Style That Belongs / Product Drop) runs the same way the customer experiences the rack: each item earns its place.
4. **Local Western Cape identity is a permanent content layer.** Paarl, Winelands, Cape Town tourism, local events, school holidays, weekend warriors. This is not "regional content" — it is *the* content for Stick's primary market.
5. **No month is owned by a single lane.** A useful weekly rhythm may exist (e.g. product on weekdays, human content mid-week) but no hard rota. Lanes layer on each other.

### Coaching (5)
1. **Coaching sells improvement, not lessons.** Always frame in terms of what the golfer will be able to do next round, not what they will "learn."
2. **TrackMan data is the proof, not the headline.** Numbers serve a conclusion, not the other way around.
3. **Group coaching + private lessons coexist.** Group = community + entry price point. Private = conversion + fitting pipeline. Both must be visible weekly.
4. **Junior coaching is a year-round lane, not a school holiday add-on.** Drive visibility through parents (the buyer).
5. **Coaching converts to fitting.** Every coaching call-to-action has a fitting pathway: "Your numbers tell a story. Book a fitting to read it with us."

### Fitting (5)
1. **Fit First. Buy Second.** Always. Fitting is the front door; retail is the result.
2. **Every fit produces a piece of content.** Before/After data is a TikTok, a Carousel, a Story. TrackMan screenshots are an editorial property.
3. **Fitting is sold in plain language, not technical jargon.** "Your 7-iron carries 145, but here's what that means for your gapping."
4. **Three fitting tiers must be visible** (Driver, Iron, Wedge, Putter, Wedge-and-Gap are all valid entry points). Lead with putter fitting for entry; iron fitting for retention; full-bag for upgrade.
5. **No fitting is "off the rack sold separately."** Every fit produces a recommendation. Recommendations must respect the player's data, not margin.

### Retail (5)
1. **The rack is a curated edit, not a catalogue.** Every product has a reason it belongs. Why-It's-Here is the editorial property that justifies every retail post.
2. **Stick stocks brands traditional SA golf shops ignore** (Takomo, Vice, L.A.B., Psycho Bunny, Miura, Avoda, Fujimoto). Curatorial edge is the brand promise. No discount theatre.
3. **Apparel and accessories (Psycho Bunny, caps, polos, socks, umbrellas, balls, bags, grips, shafts) are not an afterthought.** They are visible every week and have their own content lane (Style That Belongs).
4. **Workshop-related retail (grips, shafts, regrips, loft/lie, upgrades) is a permanent commercial message.** Built At Stick is the editorial property.
5. **Vouchers and gifting are full commercial lanes**, not "seasonal extras." Mother's Day, Father's Day, end-of-year gifting, vouchers as gifts, vouchers as a fitting-introduction gift.

### Calendar Planning Process (5)
1. **A-PIN events get 6–8 weeks of pre-planning.** No A-PIN is ever "we'll see what we have."
2. **B-PIN events get 3–4 weeks.** Enough for content + product + capture if needed.
3. **C-PIN events are Stories/Carousel only.** No full production runway needed.
4. **The monthly theme lives ABOVE the calendar, not inside every post.** Calendar items reference the theme by alignment, not by slogan.
5. **Lane cadences are configurable rules, not mandatory quotas.** The planner uses them as defaults when proposing the month but lanes can flex per quarter (e.g. golf tournament month may push fitting lane harder; gifting month may push apparel harder).

## C. SEASONAL STRATEGY (2027)

### Q1: January – March
**Seasonal job:** "New Year, New Bag." The first big swing of the year. Pre-season fit-up. Resolve the December gifting recipients and convert them into customers.

**Coaching objective:** Junior coaching wave (back-to-school), beginner-friendly content, "your swing in 2027 starts here" angle.

**Fitting objective:** Driver fitting push (longer days = full swing work), iron fitting for new-season bag builds, putter fitting for "I made a New Year resolution" moments.

**Retail objective:** Voucher redemption, giftable apparel (Psycho Bunny, caps, polos), balls + accessories refresh, "January tax-reset" angle.

**Main products/services to push:**
- Driver fitting (full swing work in summer light)
- Vouchers (redeem + new)
- Psycho Bunny apparel (Style That Belongs January push)
- Junior coaching bundles
- Balls (Vice, Titleist — new-season refresh)

**Key calendar pins:**
- Jan 1 — New Year's Day (A-PIN: "New Year New Bag")

- Feb 14 — Valentine's Day (B-PIN: gift vouchers, "buy her a lesson")

**Notes:** South African summer is peak golf season in the Western Cape. Long daylight hours = more fitting appointments. School holidays in early January drive junior coaching + parent-child lesson bundles.

### Q2: April – June
**Seasonal job:** Major-tournament season. Global golf attention spikes. SA autumn cooling → fewer casual rounds but more dedicated fittings.

**Coaching objective:** Connect global major moments to local coaching: "What the Masters tells us about your swing," "Why the US Open conditions matter for your iron fitting," "How Open Championship conditions affect your wedge choices."

**Fitting objective:** Major-themed fitting appointments. Use the tournaments as the hook: book a wedge fitting ahead of winter; book a putter fitting inspired by Masters week.

**Retail objective:** Premium product drops timed to majors (Takomo ahead of Masters, L.A.B. ahead of US Open). Balls refresh for autumn rounds. Apparel layering for cooler weather.

**Main products/services to push:**
- Major-tied putter/iron fittings
- Wedge fittings (autumn course conditions)
- Workshop upgrades (loft/lie, regrip, shaft)
- Apparel layering (long-sleeve, rain gear, jackets)

**Key calendar pins:**
- Apr 8–11 — Masters Tournament (A-PIN, global)
- Apr 27 — Freedom Day (Tuesday, no long weekend) (B-PIN)
- May 1 — Workers' Day (Saturday, observed Mon May 3) (A-PIN)
- May 9 — Mother's Day (B-PIN)
- May 13–16 — PGA Championship (A-PIN, global)
- Jun 16 — Youth Day (Wednesday) (B-PIN)
- Jun 17–20 — US Open (A-PIN, global)
- Jun 20 — Father's Day (A-PIN: biggest gift-purchase moment)

**Campaign theme:** "Major / What the Major Tells Us" — content series connecting each major to a coaching/fitting insight.

**Notes:** Father's Day is the single biggest retail + voucher moment of Q2. Father's Day campaign should run 6 weeks (mid-May onwards) and combine: fitting vouchers, premium club drops (L.A.B., Takomo), apparel bundles (Psycho Bunny for Dad). Autumn light is still strong enough for swing work; expect fitting appointments to hold.

### Q3: July – September
**Seasonal job:** Winter golf season. Cape Town is in its rainy season — indoor-friendly content (workshop, theory, fitting) becomes more important. The Open Championship and Ryder Cup anchor the global calendar.

**Coaching objective:** Theory-heavy, "what changes in your swing" content. Indoor lessons promoted. Off-season skill-building.

**Fitting objective:** Winter is workshop-heavy: re-grip, re-shaft, loft/lie, custom builds. Wedge gapping sessions (autumn/winter course conditions = more partial shots).

**Retail objective:** Workshop upgrades, replacement grips, shaft upgrades, indoor training aids (putting mats, simulators, training aids). Father's Day (Jun 20) gifting continues into early July.

**Main products/services to push:**
- Workshop services (Built At Stick)
- Re-grips, re-shafts, loft/lie
- Wedge fittings + gapping
- Indoor training aids
- Premium putter pushes (L.A.B. — global attention around Ryder Cup)

## F. CALENDAR PLANNING CHECKLIST

A practical list Christelle can use when sitting with the calendar.

### Quarterly Planning (4× per year, 8 weeks before quarter starts)
- [ ] Review previous quarter's performance data (which pillars hit their numbers, which didn't).
- [ ] Identify next quarter's A-PIN events and confirm dates.
- [ ] Set quarter's commercial focus (coaching / fitting / retail primary, secondary, tertiary).
- [ ] Set quarter's product push list (specific SKUs to feature).
- [ ] Set quarter's campaign theme (1-line strategic frame).
- [ ] Identify any new content properties to launch (e.g. "Why It's Here" carousels, "Built At Stick" Reels).
- [ ] Confirm lane cadences for the quarter (product, fitting, coaching, apparel, etc.).
- [ ] Lock monthly themes per month of the quarter.

### Monthly Planning (1× per month, 4 weeks before month starts)
- [ ] Identify all A and B pins in the month.
- [ ] Map each A-pin to: lead content, supporting content, paid media, retail push, fitting push, coaching push.
- [ ] Confirm production runway (T-21 to T-1) for each A-pin.
- [ ] Build weekly grid with parallel lanes (no day owned by one lane).
- [ ] Identify NEEDS_CAPTURE items for the month and schedule shoots.
- [ ] Identify NEEDS_DESIGN items and brief designer.
- [ ] Confirm calendar item statuses (READY / NEEDS_CAPTURE / NEEDS_DESIGN / NEEDS_APPROVAL).

### Weekly Traffic Control (1× per week)
- [ ] Going live: which items publish this week?
- [ ] Needs capture: what's still missing content?
- [ ] Needs design: what's still missing design?
- [ ] Needs approval: what's awaiting sign-off?
- [ ] Product / service push: which products are being featured this week?
- [ ] Paid support: which items are boosted?
- [ ] Blocked: what's stuck and why?

### Per A-PIN Event (8-week runway)
- [ ] **8 weeks before:** Confirm date and pillar. Set campaign theme. Identify lead content (Carousel or Reel), supporting content (Stories), retail push, fitting push, coaching push. Identify any paid media spend.
- [ ] **6 weeks before:** Brief capture (if needed). Brief designer. Confirm retail + fitting + coaching availability. Confirm any partner involvement.
- [ ] **4 weeks before:** Capture shoot (if applicable). Draft copy in brand voice. Approve hero asset.
- [ ] **2 weeks before:** All supporting assets finalised. Paid media scheduled. Retail + fitting + coaching operational.
- [ ] **Event week:** Publish. Engage with event in real time. Post-event follow-up content.
- [ ] **Week after:** Reflection content. Re-purpose event content. Document performance for next event.

### Per B-PIN Event (4-week runway)
- [ ] **4 weeks before:** Identify content hook. Identify retail/fitting/coaching angle.
- [ ] **2 weeks before:** Content drafted. Assets ready.
- [ ] **Event week:** Publish + boost as needed.
- [ ] **Week after:** Reflect if appropriate.

### Per C-PIN Event (2-week runway)
- [ ] **2 weeks before:** Stories drafted (carousel or story only).
- [ ] **Event day:** Post.
- [ ] Skip if overrun by A or B pins.

### Lane Health Check (1× per month)
- [ ] Are all lanes producing content at cadence?
- [ ] Which lanes are under-producing? Adjust cadence or repurpose content.
- [ ] Which lanes are over-producing? Check if quality is holding.
- [ ] Are gaps flagged (e.g. no human content, no fitting proof, campaign without paid support)?
- [ ] Is the monthly theme visible across lanes without being literal in every caption?

### Production Health (1× per week)
- [ ] How many NEEDS_CAPTURE items are open?
- [ ] How many NEEDS_DESIGN items are open?
- [ ] How many items are pending approval?
- [ ] Are any items approaching deadline without status update?

### Yearly Review (1× per year, late January)
- [ ] Did the big idea hold? Where did it show? Where did it drift?
- [ ] Did all three pillars stay active every month?
- [ ] Which recurring properties produced the most engagement?
- [ ] Which A-PIN events hit, which underperformed, which were demoted?
- [ ] What worked commercially? What didn't?
- [ ] Adjust lane cadences for the new year.
- [ ] Refresh product push list.
- [ ] Identify new content properties to test.
