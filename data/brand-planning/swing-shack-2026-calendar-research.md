## A. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Swing Shack operates as a **single indoor-golf footprint** at the heart of Johannesburg's premium Randburg/Randpark golf catchment. The shop is not on a high street, it is **inside Randpark estate** — three minutes from both the Firethorn and Bushwillow courses. That geography is the spine's load-bearing constraint.

### Key strategic principles applied to swing-shack specifically

- **All 11 swing-shack lanes run every week.** No month is owned by one lane. Tracked in `swing-shack.json`.
- **The big idea ("Golf is more fun when it makes sense") is the consistency, not the caption.** swing-shack does not restate it on every post. The big idea is visible in *what* is posted (clear, useful, data-backed) and *how* (calm, friendly, demystifying, no nonsense). Captions stay plain.
- **TrackMan 4 + first-in-SA TrackMan iO is a permanent content layer.** Not a launch stunt. Every swing-shack lane (swing-truth, fitting, coaching) routes through the iO. The iO makes the "data over hype" promise real.
- **Old Parktonian Sports Club membership is a parallel buyer pool** — swing-shack shares a venue with other Parktonian sports; a sports-membership cross-promo lane is available but not yet canonical (operator to confirm).
- **JOBURG-specific, not Winelands.** Stick's spine leans on Paarl/Stellenbosch/Franschhoek. Swing-shack's spine mirrors with Randpark/Bordeaux/Ruimsig/Bryanston. No Boland crossover.
- **Non-golf public moments are first-class spine events** if they fit the swing-shack community profile (LGE Election Day, Heritage Day, school terms). Voting + community is consistent with swing-shack's "data, truth, no nonsense" lane voice.
- **Stripped events (with reasoning)**: Day of Goodwill (operator instruction — doesn't read true), Halloween (no SA public-holiday status, audience mismatch). Modifications: December School Holiday / Family Golf moved to 2026-12-09 to align with confirmed Gauteng Term 4 holiday end.

## B. NON-NEGOTIABLES

### Overall Swing Shack Strategy

1. **The big idea is the consistency, not the caption.** swing-shack does not restate "Golf is more fun when it makes sense" on every post. The big idea is visible in *what* is posted (calm, useful, data-backed, demystifying) and *how* (plain, friendly, no nonsense). Captions stay plain.
2. **All 11 lanes run every week, every month, every quarter.** swing-truth, truth-about, 10-ball-truth, shack-sessions, practice, fitting, coaching, membership, paid, search, crm — *simultaneously* active. No exceptions for "slow months".
3. **Every fitting session produces content.** Before/After TrackMan data is a TikTok, a Carousel, a Story. The iO shot data is an editorial property.
4. **Old Parktonian Sports Club is the venue, Randpark is the catchment.** swing-shack's primary market is the Randpark membership + Randburg / Bordeaux / Ferndale / Linden residential golfers + Paulshof / Rivonia weekend spillover from Sandton. That catchment does not include Boland wine-tourists — geography differs from Stick.
5. **No month is owned by a single lane.** A useful weekly rhythm may exist (e.g. fitting mid-week, weekend play + lessons) but no hard rota. Lanes layer.

### Fitting (4)

1. **Fit First. Buy Second.** Always. Fitting is the front door; retail is the result. swing-shack's stock (Takomo, Vice, L.A.B., LA Golf, Miura) is what the bay is built for.
2. **Every fit produces a piece of content.** Before/After TrackMan 4 data is a TikTok, a Carousel, a Story. iO shot data is an editorial property.
3. **Fitting is sold in plain language, not technical jargon.** "Your 7-iron carries 145, but here's what that means for your gapping."
4. **The TrackMan iO removes the markers-on-floor ritual.** swing-shack's positioning is "walk in, hit balls, get data" — no calibration dance.

### Coaching (4)

1. **Coaching sells improvement, not lessons.** Always frame in terms of what the golfer will be able to do next round, not what they will "learn."
2. **TrackMan data is the proof, not the headline.** Numbers serve a conclusion, not the other way.
3. **Group coaching + private lessons coexist.** Group = community + entry price. Private = conversion + fitting pipeline.
4. **Junior coaching is a year-round lane.** Drive visibility through parents (the buyer). Term 2 holiday + Term 3 holiday + Term 4 holiday + saints-festival weekend = the four slots where school-age juniors show up.

### Retail (4)

1. **The rack is a curated edit, not a catalogue.** Every product has a reason it belongs.
2. **Swing Shack stocks premium brands traditional SA golf shops ignore.** Takomo, Vice, L.A.B., Miura — curatorial edge is the brand promise. No discount theatre.
3. **Apparel and accessories are not an afterthought.** Caps, polos, socks, balls, bags are visible every week.
4. **Workshop-related retail (grips, shafts, regrips) is a permanent commercial message.**

### Calendar Planning Process

1. **A-PIN events get 6–8 weeks of pre-planning.** Joburg Ladies Open + BFCM + Festive Gifting + New Year all qualify.
2. **B-PIN events get 3–4 weeks.** Saints Festival + LGE Election Day + December School Holiday.
3. **C-PIN events are Stories/Carousel only.** Heritage Day, Presidents Cup, Singles' Day, Day of Reconciliation.
4. **The shopping-month focus lives ABOVE the calendar.** Calendar items reference by alignment, not by slogan.
5. **Lane cadences are configurable rules, not mandatory quotas.**
