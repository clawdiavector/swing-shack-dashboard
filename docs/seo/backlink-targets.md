Google does not show a public "verified" label, so verification itself is not proven from outside, but owner posts and replies need a claimed profile. Things to fix before building more links on it:

- **The 23 Sep owner post says Swing Shack is "right here in Linden".** The venue is in Bordeaux. It also says "Free swing analysis on first visit", which is a claim nobody has signed off.
- **Postcode (still open):** Google says 2196, the website's search data says 2194.
- **Venue name:** same place (Christelle confirmed), but the website's search data says "Old Parks Sports Club" while the footer and Google say Old Parktonian. Pick one spelling and use it everywhere, because directories match on the exact text.
- **Area:** Randburg is in Johannesburg (Christelle confirmed). Use "Bordeaux, Randburg, Johannesburg" in listings so both searches match.

A citation is only worth building if name, address and phone match everywhere, so settle these first.

## Tier 4: earned links (slower, higher value)

- **Data posts.** A short piece of real, anonymised TrackMan data from Swing Shack (for example, what the average first-session numbers look like) is the type of thing golf writers link to. Needs your OK on what data to share. I will not make numbers up.
- **Events.** A fitting day or a clinic with a partner brand. The partner's page links back.
- **Competitors' gap.** Ubersuggest lists 742 keywords randpark.co.za ranks for that Swing Shack does not. A look at the pages that earn them will show which topics attract links in South Africa. Needs a fresh Ubersuggest login on this PC, which only you can do.

## Not recommended

- Buying links or paying for guest-post packages. They put the site at risk with Google.
- Mass directory submissions. They add low-quality links and no traffic.
- Commenting links on blogs or forums. See the community scout for honest ways to be present on Reddit and GolfWRX.
