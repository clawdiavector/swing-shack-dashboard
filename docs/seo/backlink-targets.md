# Swing Shack backlink targets

Status: first list, 2026-10-20. Nothing here has been contacted or submitted.

**Where Swing Shack stands** (Ubersuggest, 18 Sep 2026, then 33 links from 15 domains on the live SEO page): domain authority 12. Almost all links are nofollow. Competitors with real authority: randpark.co.za (30), theproshop.co.za (38), worldofgolf.co.za (24), modgolf.co.za (14).

**How to read this list.** I know these kinds of sites exist. I have not opened each one, so every row says what to verify first. Do the Tier 1 rows before anything else, they are free, fast and fully in your control.

## Tier 1: listings you control (do first)

| Where | Why | Verify first |
|---|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | The main lever for "near me" and Randburg searches. Name, address, hours, categories, photos | Whether the profile exists and is verified. A GBP drafts doc is already in `docs/gbp-drafts-for-review-2026-10-15.md` |
| Bing Places | Feeds Bing and some AI answers | Whether a listing exists |
| Apple Business Connect | Apple Maps | Whether a listing exists |
| Facebook page, Instagram bio, LinkedIn page | Profile links back to swingshack.co.za | Each bio uses the full https://swingshack.co.za link |
| Local directories (Yellow Pages SA, Brabys, Hotfrog, Cylex) | Citations. Most are nofollow, they still confirm name, address and phone | Name, address and phone must match the Google profile exactly |

## Tier 2: brands Swing Shack sells and has written about

Dealer or stockist pages are real backlinks. The site already has posts on all of these, so the relationship exists.

| Brand | Ask | Verify first |
|---|---|---|
| Takomo | Stockist or "where to buy" page in South Africa | Whether they list SA dealers |
| Vice Golf | Same | Same |
| Miura and Fujimoto | Same, plus a mention of the comparison post | Who the SA distributor is |
| Avoda | Same, plus the same-length irons post | Same |

## Tier 4: earned links (slower, higher value)

- **Data posts.** A short piece of real, anonymised TrackMan data from Swing Shack (for example, what the average first-session numbers look like) is the type of thing golf writers link to. Needs your OK on what data to share. I will not make numbers up.
- **Events.** A fitting day or a clinic with a partner brand. The partner's page links back.
- **Competitors' gap.** Ubersuggest lists 742 keywords randpark.co.za ranks for that Swing Shack does not. A look at the pages that earn them will show which topics attract links in South Africa. Needs a fresh Ubersuggest login on this PC, which only you can do.

## Not recommended

- Buying links or paying for guest-post packages. They put the site at risk with Google.
- Mass directory submissions. They add low-quality links and no traffic.
- Commenting links on blogs or forums. See the community scout for honest ways to be present on Reddit and GolfWRX.
