# SEO fix pack, 28 October 2026 (Swing Shack and Stick)

From the daily site check, after removing its false findings (PR #255). Every text below comes from the page's own wording or from facts Christelle confirmed, and every figure says where it comes from. All of it is done in WordPress (Elementor or Yoast). Once the Yoast snippet is installed on a site, the title and description fixes can also be done from Campaign OS > Results > SEO.

## Swing Shack (swingshack.co.za)

### 1. Give each page one main heading (H1). High priority.
The big title on each page is styled text, not a heading, so Google sees no main heading. In Elementor, click the title, then under **HTML Tag** (or "Title tag") choose **H1**, then Update.

| Page | The title to set to H1 |
|---|---|
| Homepage | "Coaching, Fitting, Practice & Play" (currently H2) |
| Membership | "Join the Club. Play Smarter. Train Better." |
| Club Fitting | The top title that starts "Club Fitting" and ends "Golf, Made Personal" |

Only one H1 per page.

### 2. Add a short "Questions" section to each page
Add a heading **Questions** (H2), then each question as an **H3** with the answer as normal text under it. Questions must start with What, How, Can, Is, Where or Why, which is also what AI answers pick up.

**Homepage**
- **Where is Swing Shack?** At Old Parktonian Sports Club, 1 Garden Road, Bordeaux, Randburg, Johannesburg. There is ample parking.
- **What are your opening hours?** Monday to Saturday 9 am to 9 pm, Sunday 9 am to 5 pm.
- **Can I walk in?** Yes, walk-ins are welcome, but a bay cannot be guaranteed. Book online to be sure of your spot.
- **Can I hire clubs?** Yes, clubs can be hired on request. If you have your own, bring them.
- **What can I do at Swing Shack?** Coaching, club fitting, practice and play, backed by TrackMan data.

**Membership** (from the page itself)
- **What do members get?** Four free practice sessions a month, three free loft and lie checks a year, 15% off lessons, packages, social play and practice, 25% off club fittings, and member clinics and workshops.
- **How do I become a member?** Contact us with the form on this page.

**Club Fitting** (prices and times from the booking page, read 26 October; link the answers to `/bookings/`)
- **How long does a club fitting take?** Ball, putter, and loft and lie fittings take 60 minutes. Driver, iron, wedge, and fairway or hybrid fittings take 90 minutes. A full bag fitting takes 3 hours.
- **What does a club fitting cost?** R800 for a ball, putter, or loft and lie fitting; R1,000 for a driver, iron, wedge, or fairway and hybrid fitting; R1,500 for combined fittings; R2,000 for a full bag. Current prices are on the booking page.
- **Is there a free assessment?** Yes, a free 30-minute club and swing assessment can be booked on the booking page.
- **Do members pay less?** Yes, members get 25% off club fittings.

Check the prices on the booking page before publishing; they change.

### 2. One image without alt text on each page
It is the small arrow icon on the buttons (`button_icon.svg`). It is decorative, so give it an **empty** alt (`alt=""`). It sits in the theme's button settings or an HTML widget, so it may need someone comfortable with the theme. Low priority.
