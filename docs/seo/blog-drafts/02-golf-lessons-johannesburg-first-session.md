# Golf Lessons in Johannesburg: What Your First TrackMan Lesson Covers

A good first lesson answers one question: what is your swing actually doing? At Swing Shack, TrackMan answers it with numbers and a coach turns the numbers into one thing to work on.

This is what the first session covers, so you know what to expect before you book.

## Why start with data

Most golfers learn by feel. Feel is useful, but it is not always accurate. What you feel you did and what the club did can be different things.

TrackMan measures what the club and the ball did. The coach starts there, so the lesson is built on what happened and not on a guess.

## What the coach looks at first

On the first lesson, a coach usually starts with a handful of figures:

- **Club path and face angle.** These two together explain most slices and hooks.
- **Attack angle.** Whether you hit down or up into the ball.
- **Strike quality.** Smash factor shows how cleanly the ball was struck.
- **Dispersion.** Where the ball lands across a set of shots, not just the best one.

## How the session runs

1. **You hit some shots.** No pressure and no performance. The coach watches the numbers.
2. **The coach picks one thing.** Not ten. One change you can understand and repeat.
3. **You try the change and watch the numbers move.** You see in the same session whether it worked.
4. **You leave knowing what to practise.** And why.

Sessions are 30 or 60 minutes. See the [lesson options](/product-category/lesson/) or go straight to the [bookings page](/bookings/).

## Is this for beginners

Yes. A first-timer gets the same welcome and the same bay as a low handicapper. A beginner can start with the basics of strike and direction. A better player can work on a specific miss.

## Beginner, improver or returning player

- **Just starting.** Ask for a session that covers grip, set-up and a clean strike first.
- **Playing but stuck.** Bring the miss you want to fix. A slice and a thin strike have different causes.
- **Coming back after a break.** Use the first session to see where your swing is now.

## Lessons and clubs go together

If the numbers show that your clubs are working against your swing, the coach will say so. A [club fitting](/club-fitting/) can follow the lesson. Swing Shack fits the club to the player, not the other way round.
