# Indoor Golf in Johannesburg: What a Session at Swing Shack Looks Like

You hit a real ball into a screen. TrackMan reads what the ball did. That is the whole idea, and it is more useful than it sounds.

If you have searched "indoor golf Johannesburg" and wondered what you would be walking into, this is the first visit, step by step.

## What indoor golf is

Indoor golf means hitting a real golf ball, with your own clubs, in a bay instead of on a range. A launch monitor measures the swing and the ball flight. A screen shows where the ball would have gone.

It is not a video game. The ball is real and so is the swing. The difference is that every shot comes back with numbers.

## What TrackMan measures

TrackMan is a radar launch monitor. On every shot it reports figures such as:

- **Club head speed.** How fast the club is moving at impact.
- **Ball speed.** How fast the ball leaves the face.
- **Smash factor.** Ball speed divided by club head speed. It shows how cleanly you struck it.
- **Attack angle.** Whether the club is moving up or down into the ball.
- **Club path and face angle.** Together these explain a slice or a hook.
- **Carry distance.** How far the ball flies before it lands.

You do not need to know any of these before you arrive. The numbers are there to answer one question: why did that ball do that?

## What a first visit looks like

1. **You book a session.** Sessions are 30 or 60 minutes. Book on the [bookings page](/bookings/).
2. **You warm up in the bay.** Bring your own clubs, or ask us to hire you a set.
3. **You hit balls and watch the numbers.** The screen shows each shot. Ask what any number means, that is part of the point.
4. **You leave with something specific.** One thing you now know about your swing that you did not know when you walked in.

Every level gets the same welcome. A first-timer and a single-figure player get the same bay and the same respect.

## Why people use a bay instead of a range

- **The weather does not matter.** Rain, heat and wind do not move your session.
- **The number is the feedback.** On a range you guess where the ball finished. In a bay you read it.
- **You can see change.** Make an adjustment, hit five balls, see what moved.

## Coaching, fitting or practice

A bay works three ways, and it helps to know which you want:

- **Practice.** You hit balls and read the numbers yourself.
- **Coaching.** A coach reads the data with you and picks one thing to work on.
- **Fitting.** The data decides which clubs suit your swing. See [club fitting](/club-fitting/), and why [indoor fittings work](/indoor-vs-outdoor-club-fitting/).
