# Swing Shack — visual language (canonical)

Positioning: performance golf, TrackMan truth, coaching and fitting seriousness,
"where good golfers get better". Avoid entertainment-bar golf cliches, neon
cyber-golf, stock golfer-grin energy, Canva-style template slideshows.

Composition: restraint over decoration; generous negative space; one clear focal
subject; shadows and silhouette for depth; premium sports-apparel campaign feel;
photo-real or near photo-real for people and simulators.

Anti-slop: no oversaturated HDR, no six fonts, no default-gradient look; if it
reads obviously AI, reject.

Copy split: on-image text = hook/stat/proof, numbers-led; caption is written
separately and must not duplicate overlay copy.

Market: metres not yards; never invent prices or offers.
