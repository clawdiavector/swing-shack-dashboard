# Marketing OS — North Star

> The product spec that should drive every Campaign OS decision. Source of truth for "what does Marketing OS mean by X".

## Core idea

Every piece of information should move through this chain:

**DATA → INTERPRETATION → EXPLANATION → RECOMMENDATION → CREATION → ACTION → RESULT → LEARNING**

Meta, Google Analytics, GMB, Ubersuggest, creative analysis, sales/conversion — all connect through this chain instead of being looked at separately.

## Feel

Not a dashboard. A **very good Head of Marketing inside the software** saying:

> "Here is what happened. Here is why. Here is what I think you should do next. Here is the campaign I would run. Here is the creative I would use. Here is the evidence behind that decision."

## Home screen principle

When you open the OS in the morning, you should NOT see 40 charts. You should see something like:

**GOOD MORNING — Your biggest opportunity today**

> "Club fitting searches increased 31% this week, but your last fitting post was 18 days ago. Your previous fitting posts containing a golfer + TrackMan screen generated 2.4× more booking-page visits than product-only graphics.
>
> Recommended action: Run a fitting education post today.
>
> Suggested hook: 'Your driver might not be the problem.'
>
> I've prepared the caption, image concept and CTA."

Underneath: **What worked / What didn't / What to repeat / What to stop / What needs attention / What Marketing OS has prepared**.

## Intelligence engine — 5 questions every insight answers

1. **What happened?** — basic analytics layer ("Reach increased 18%.")
2. **Why did it happen?** — analysis layer ("Most of the increase came from two Reels. Both opened with a golfer problem rather than a product introduction.")
3. **Why does that matter?** — marketing understanding ("Problem-led content appears to attract golfers who don't already follow Swing Shack.")
4. **What should we do?** — useful layer ("Produce two more problem-led fitting Reels this week.")
5. **How confident are we?** — honesty layer ("High confidence. The pattern appeared in 8 of your last 11 educational Reels.")

This pattern should exist **throughout the OS**, not just on the home screen.

## Creative intelligence

The OS should literally **see every image and video** and extract:

- Background, person, product, branding, text overlay
- Typography (font, weight, size, alignment, contrast)
- Composition (rule of thirds, negative space, headline position)
- Lighting, mood, marketing role (education / fitting / problem awareness)

Then connect to performance:

> "Images showing the actual Swing Shack environment outperform generic golf imagery by 64% for profile actions."

> "Posts where TrackMan is clearly visible generate more fitting enquiries than posts where the technology is implied."

> "Close-up equipment shots perform well for saves but poorly for bookings."

> "Your strongest fitting ads usually contain a human, visible technology and a problem-focused headline."

**That's visual learning.**

## Visual DNA (per brand)

Every business gets its own visual brain:

- What the bays look like, the entrance, floors, equipment positions
- What the Zen Swing Stage looks like, wall surfaces
- Which people commonly appear, which backgrounds are usable
- Which images are hero images, good backgrounds, objects that can be cut out
- Which images should **never** be used because they don't look like the brand

When generating, the prompt should not be:

> "Generate premium indoor golf facility."

It should be:

> "Use Swing Shack Bay 3 as the environmental reference. Maintain the black ceiling, turf position, screen proportions and warm lighting. Use the golfer cutout from Asset 428. Preserve the brand composition style from the best-performing fitting creative."

## Image generator

Should NOT begin with a blank prompt box. Should begin with:

**What are we trying to achieve?** (Booking / Awareness / Education / Product sale / Event registration)

Then the OS decides what creative structure fits that goal, picks an audience, surfaces the current insight, recommends a concept + visual recipe + hook, and **creates three concepts based on known winning visual patterns** — not random AI pictures.

## Caption intelligence

Not "Generate Caption." Instead:

**Caption Strategy**

- Goal (e.g. "booking conversion")
- Recommended structure (Problem → explanation → credibility → action)
- Then writes Hook / Body / Proof / CTA

And the OS explains **why** each choice was made:

> "I avoided 'unlock your potential' because generic aspirational phrases historically perform poorly for this brand."

> "I used 'your driver' rather than 'golfers' because direct second-person hooks produce higher stop rates."

> "I did not open with Swing Shack because brand-first openings underperform problem-first openings."

## Hook Lab

The OS should learn different hook mechanics:

- Curiosity, Contradiction, Loss aversion, Mistake, Identity, Specific result
- Myth busting, Problem recognition, Unexpected truth, Comparison
- Challenge, Proof

And understand **when each works**. Example progression:

- Weak: *"Improve your golf with Swing Shack."*
- Better: *"Your clubs might be costing you shots."*
- Better still: *"If your 7-iron and 6-iron carry almost the same distance, you have a problem."*

The OS should know **why** each upgrade is better.

## South African intelligence

Mandatory market context layer. The OS must know:

- Metres, not yards. Rands, not dollars.
- Johannesburg seasonality, loadshedding implications, public holidays
- School holidays, SA sporting events, golf culture
- Local clubs, terminology, humour, price sensitivity, competitors
- Local weather, SA spelling/English

It must **reject** ideas that technically work in American marketing but feel wrong here.

## Campaign builder (one of the main experiences)

User says: **"I need more club fittings in September."**

OS asks only what it genuinely needs, then builds:

- Campaign objective
- Audience definition
- Core problem statement
- Campaign idea (e.g. "STOP GUESSING.")
- Campaign architecture: hero concept, core message, offer, audience segments, content pillars, 30-day calendar, Reels / static / carousels / stories / GMB / landing page / Google search opportunities, captions, visual recipes, hooks, CTAs, remarketing content, measurement plan
- **Why this campaign** — the reasoning, not just the deliverable

## Campaign memory

After the campaign ends, it should NOT disappear. The OS should say:

> "STOP GUESSING produced 43 fitting enquiries."

Then analyse: best hook, best creative, best format, best audience, weakest piece, and the learning — which becomes part of the next campaign. The OS **gets smarter**.

## Performance screen

Above every chart: **What you're looking at / What changed / Why it probably changed / What that means / What I would do next.**

Example for website traffic:

> 1,843 users ↑ 22%
>
> **What caused it**: Instagram contributed most of the increase, particularly two educational Reels.
>
> **What matters**: Traffic from educational content stayed on the fitting pages 41% longer than traffic from product content.
>
> **Recommendation**: Increase fitting education content from one to three pieces per week.

That's analytics a normal person can actually use.

## Opportunity Finder

The OS constantly looks across everything (Google search, Meta performance, website traffic, GMB calls, competitor gaps) and surfaces:

# OPPORTUNITY DETECTED

**Club fitting education** — Score: 91/100

> "Search demand + proven content performance + weak competitor education + strong conversion behaviour."

**BUILD CAMPAIGN** — one button.

## Marketing rules engine

The OS maintains a constantly evolving set of rules (not absolute laws):

- Problem-focused hooks generally outperform company announcements
- Specific claims outperform vague aspiration
- Don't use jargon before creating relevance
- CTA intensity should match audience awareness
- Don't ask for purchase before explaining value
- Showing real environments increases credibility for physical businesses

And learns brand-specific overrides:

> "These rules work differently for Swing Shack."

**General marketing knowledge + SA market knowledge + Golf-industry knowledge + Swing Shack-specific evidence = the recommendation.**

## The big difference

Most marketing software answers: **"What happened?"**

Marketing OS should answer: **"What happened, why did it happen, why does it matter, what should we do about it, and can I do that for you now?"**

That is the north star.
