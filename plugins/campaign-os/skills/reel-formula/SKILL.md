# The reel formula

Christelle's third formula (30 Oct 2026), after the poster and post formulas
(`visual-design/formulas.md`). Full sources, ranked by trust: wiki
`research/reel-formula-2026-10-30`. Interactive page with a playing demo and a scorer:
https://claude.ai/artifact/GxXAmX4zC1po5foqR9Kp4Q

> **Reel = Native × (Hook + Story + Rhythm + Sound + Brand) × Ask**

Native and Ask are gates (yes or no); one "no" makes the reel a zero. The five factors score 0, 1 or 2.
**Post at 8 or more, with no factor at 0.** Still frames inside a reel (the hook frame, the end card) also
pass the poster formula.

## The seven parts

| Part | Rule | Check |
|---|---|---|
| **Native** (gate) | 9:16, 1080×1920 or more. Reels: keep the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% each side free of text and logos. Stories: top 14%, bottom 20%. Original (no other app's watermark), real people, filmed the way people film; doesn't look like an ad. **Ads use real human video only.** | Lay the safe zone over the frame. Does anything important sit under the buttons? |
| **Hook** | Offer clear by 3 s, hook landed by 6 s. Motion in the first frame. Niche and specific, so the right golfer feels seen. No logo intro, no warm-up | Muted, first 2 seconds only: would a golfer in Paarl or Johannesburg stop? |
| **Story** | One job per reel (aware, teach or act). A turn ("but"), never a feature list. Shapes: problem, turn, payoff · hook, demo, ask · claim, proof, offer · before and after | Point to the "but". No turn means it's a list |
| **Rhythm** | Challenges and fitting sessions 0.7–1.5 s a shot; product or brand films hold 2.5 s or more; cuts come in bursts, then a hold. On-screen text at most 5–10 words a second. Stories: something changes every 1.5–2 s | Scrub it. Is any 3-second stretch unchanged, and is that on purpose? |
| **Sound** | Clean voice or licensed music, never AI music (`add_sound.py` with a licensed track). Captions burned in. It must make sense both muted and with sound | Watch once muted and once with sound |
| **Brand** | Brand or product visible by 3 s and woven through, not stamped at the end. **One brand per reel.** Repeat what people recognise (a series, a face, a line) | Pause at 3 s: whose reel is it? |
| **Ask** (gate) | One action worded around the outcome, shown on screen and said. Postiz stories carry no link sticker: Swing Shack says "link in bio" (bio goes to bookings), Stick says "DM us". The offer itself is named on screen ("Free club assessment") | Could a stranger do the next step without the caption? |

## Time shape (15–20 s)

Hook 0–3 s → the turn ("but") 3–6 s → proof 6–11 s (real bay, real club, any number with its source) →
payoff → one ask. Average watch time to aim for: 11 s or more (a practitioner number until we measure ours).

Lengths by job (starting points, not benchmarks): awareness 6–15 s, direct ask under 20 s, product demo
15–30 s, teaching or fitting 20–40 s. Run two lengths side by side and let completion rate decide.

## Reading the numbers, in order

1. People leave in the first seconds → fix the hook; keep the rest the same while testing.
2. They watch but don't click or DM → fix the offer or the ask.
3. They click but don't book → fix the landing page or the audience.
4. Good, then a slow drop → creative fatigue; add new versions to the same ad set (3–5 creatives per ad group).

Hook rate = 3-second views ÷ impressions. DM shares per reach are how a reel reaches non-followers.

## Still in force

Licensed music only, never insult the golfer, every number has a source link, one brand per reel,
confident not needy, Postiz times at least 5 minutes ahead. Anything photoreal in the reel also passes
`reality-check`. Fake engagement ("get friends to comment") is out.

## How to show a reel

Give the score in one line: "Reel 9/10: native yes, hook 2, story 2, rhythm 1, sound 2, brand 2, ask: DM
CHECK", name the weakest part and what the next version changes.
