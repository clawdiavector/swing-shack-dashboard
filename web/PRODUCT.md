# Campaign Heroes

register: product

## Product purpose

Campaign Heroes is the campaigner desk for Swing Shack, Stick, and Bag Drop.
Marketers run the week from it. A CEO should be able to open Daily and know
what is waiting, what shipped, and what it did — without learning layer names.

## Users

- Christelle / campaigner — brief, inbox, studio, schedule, go live
- Kyle / CEO — waiting on you, this week, pipeline, brands, what shipped

## Tone

Marketing-first, CEO-readable. Keywords: Today, This week, Waiting on you,
Do this now, Inbox, Studio, Schedule, Go live, What worked.

Avoid on the primary rail: Jobs, Layers, Fleet, Herman, SEO Stack, Tenant Isolation.

## Anti-references

- 40-item tool lists
- Engineer layer labels (L1–L8) as navigation
- Neon cyber dashboards
- Deleting leftover pages before Kyle reviews them
