Privacy Policy

Campaign OS · Operator: Swing Shack (Johannesburg, South Africa) · Effective immediately

What this app does

Campaign OS is a marketing analytics dashboard operated by Swing Shack (the indoor golf and custom-fitting business based at 27 9th Street, Wynberg, Johannesburg). The app reads engagement metrics, post insights, and audience comments from Swing Shack's Facebook Page (facebook.com/swing.shack) and Instagram Business account (@swing_shack_jhb) so that Swing Shack staff can review content performance and plan future posts.

What data we access

The app reads the following data from Facebook and Instagram, only for accounts owned or managed by Swing Shack:

  • Facebook Page profile (name, fan count, page views)
  • Facebook Page posts (content, publish time, reactions, shares)
  • Aggregated Facebook Page insights (post engagements, view counts)
  • Comments on Facebook Page posts (commenter name, comment text, timestamp)
  • Instagram Business account profile (username, follower count, post count)
  • Instagram posts (caption, media type, publish time, engagement metrics)
  • Comments on Instagram posts

What we do NOT do

  • We never post, reply, hide, delete, or modify content on Facebook or Instagram.
  • We never share data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • We never use the data for ad targeting, retargeting, or audience profiling.
  • We never store Facebook or Instagram passwords.
  • We never sell or trade the data.

Where data is stored

All data is stored locally on infrastructure controlled by Swing Shack. Specifically: a Railway-hosted application server, a GitHub private repository for version history, and an encrypted backup on the operator's local Mac. No data leaves this infrastructure. No third-party analytics services receive the data.

Who can access the data

Only the Swing Shack business owner (Christelle) and authorised staff with login credentials to the Campaign OS dashboard. Access logs are kept on the application server. No external parties have access.

How long data is retained

Engagement metrics and post data are retained indefinitely for trend analysis. Raw API responses are cached for up to 24 hours then discarded. Comment data is retained for review but can be purged on request.

How to revoke access or request data deletion

You can revoke Campaign OS's access at any time via Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. To request deletion of all data held by Campaign OS, email info@stickgolf.co.za — we complete deletion within 7 business days.

Data breaches

In the event of a data breach affecting Campaign OS, Swing Shack will notify affected users within 72 hours via email.

Children's privacy

Campaign OS is a business tool. It does not knowingly collect data from users under 18.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be announced via the dashboard's notification system at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change.
