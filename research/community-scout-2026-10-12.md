## 1. Why do I have two golf swings (sim vs real course)

- Brand: Swing Shack (also useful for Stick)
- Platform: GolfWRX, Instruction & Academy
- Link: https://forums.golfwrx.com/topic/2111902-why-do-i-have-two-golf-swings/
- Age / replies: posted Saturday 2026-10-10, 12 replies when checked
- Why worth joining: home Uneekor/GSPro user, solid on the sim and range, falls apart on
  the course; asks directly for drills and pre-shot routine. This is the "sim to course"
  conversation Swing Shack owns.
- Rules check: instruction forum, open thread, no promotion needed
- Mode: value-only
- Draft:

  > That gap is really common, and it usually isn't mechanics. A mat and a screen give you a
  > flat lie, a known target and no consequence, so the swing stays the same but the setup
  > doesn't. Two things worth testing. First, on the course do the exact routine you use on
  > the sim: same number of looks, same waggle, same tempo from address to takeaway. A lot of
  > people rush the first tee because the routine is shorter than the one they use at home.
  > Second, hit your first ten range balls with a target and a consequence, for example you
  > must hit three fairway-width shots in a row before you leave the range. If that falls
  > apart too, it's the pressure, not the swing. If it holds up, the routine is the thing.
  > Also check your contact: the toe hits you describe can come from standing slightly
  > farther from the ball on grass than on a mat.

- Why it won't read as a pitch: no brand, no link, no product. Answers the question and gives two tests.

## 2. Signs you need to move to a lighter shaft (indoor vs outdoor fittings argument)

- Brand: Stick (fitting) and Swing Shack
- Platform: GolfWRX, Instruction & Academy
- Link: https://forums.golfwrx.com/topic/2111275-signs-you-need-to-move-to-a-lighter-shaft/
- Age / replies: active 2026-10-09 to 2026-10-10, 36 replies
- Why worth joining: members are arguing whether indoor fittings are as reliable as
  outdoor ones. Fitting credibility is Stick's territory.
- Rules check: open thread, but two members are in a heated back-and-forth. Low priority.
  Joining mid-argument risks looking like a side-taker. Only join with a neutral point.
- Mode: value-only, only if you want to
- Draft:

  > Both sides have a point. Indoor takes weather out, which helps comparing heads and
  > shafts back to back. Outdoor shows you the real flight and how the ball reacts to wind
  > and turf. The fittings I'd trust do both: pick the spec indoors where the numbers are
  > repeatable, then confirm it with a handful of shots outside before you buy. And
  > whichever you use, ask the fitter to show you a few same-club repeats so you can see how
  > far the numbers move on their own.
