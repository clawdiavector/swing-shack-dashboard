### Findings

#### Swing Consistency Discussions
Reddit discussions on swing consistency highlight:
- **Pre-shot routine importance** - repetitive routines help automate the swing
- **Transition work** - noticing when transition gets sloppy is key
- **Limited practice swings** - over-practicing before shots creates tension
- **Consistent practice with purpose** - beating balls with focus beats mindless range sessions

#### Launch Monitor Interest
- TrackMan optimizer functions calculate optimal dynamics for your club speed
- Wedge matrix building is highlighted as high-value use of launch monitor time
- Indoor TrackMan sessions require specific ball setup (Pro V1 RCT recommended for indoor use)

#### Equipment & Fitting
- New Cobra 3DP MB 5-iron getting attention for forgiveness
- Driver fitting can change everything for inconsistent strikers
- Club gapping questions remain popular (bridging gaps between irons/hybrids)

#### Indoor Golf Growth
- TGL bringing simulator golf to mainstream attention
- Questions about best use of limited TrackMan time (30 min/day)
- Garage sim builds continue to be popular DIY projects
